### **< STEP 04: IDENTITY & INCLUSIVITY >**
Embrace absolute realism. *TSI* is a grounded project where the population of Planet Omnia naturally reflects the vast diversity of the real world. Characters can be gay, lesbian, bisexual, transgender, non-binary, or anywhere along the LGBTQ+ spectrum. These identities are normal, native elements of the universe and are integrated directly into the core storyline.

### **< STEP 05: FACTION ROLES & AFFILIATIONS >**
Determine your operational role on the battlefield. You have absolute freedom to become an **Antagonist** allied directly with Axian’s forces, a completely autonomous **Neutral Survivor**, or a **Hero** fighting on the frontlines to save Planet Omnia.

### **< STEP 06: CULLING IMPACT & BACKGROUND >**
Detail your character's history, emphasizing how **Axian** directly interfered with their life during the global catastrophe known as the **Great Culling**. Axian's assault disrupted the majority of Omnia's population. If they originate from the *Everlasting Radiance Kingdom*, reflect the tragic destruction of that realm or its subsequent, stubborn recovery.

### **< STEP 07: THE DRIVING FORCE >**
Define your character's singular focus. Every OC must possess a definitive, overarching dream, personal milestone, or objective that they are actively striving to fulfill across the span of the narrative.

### **< STEP 08: PSYCHOLOGICAL GROUNDING & REALISM >**
Because Omnia has survived world-ending horrors, your character is permitted to suffer from deep physical or psychological trauma. However, you must provide a highly detailed, realistic lore justification. You must define exactly how this trauma disrupts their decision-making, complicates their elemental mastery, and hinders or shapes their survival.

### **< STEP 09: ENERGY SPECS & POWER CONTROLS >**
Design custom combat abilities and special powers derived strictly from your chosen element(s).

> [!CAUTION] **METAPHYSICAL LIMITS & POWER SCALING**
> Unlimited or cost-free healing is strictly prohibited. All techniques actively deplete **Elemental Energy**, which is a finite resource. Rare capabilities (such as healing/regeneration) require severe, foundational lore penalties and strict justification.

### **< STEP 10: FORGED EQUIPMENT & CUSTOM GEAR >**
Forge custom signature weaponry or specialized armor tailored to your character's combat style. You must state what specific elemental materials compose the gear *(e.g., "A longsword forged from High-Grade Polish Ice-Glass," "Heavy plating reinforced with Fire-Kingdom Magma-Stone," or "A spear tipped in pure Void-steel")*.

### **< STEP 11: WORLD EXPANSION & SANDBOX LORE >**
You are fully encouraged to expand this universe. Feel free to design original sub-factions, local mercenary guilds, specific regional traditions, specialized architectural styles, or even distinct localized cultural dialects spoken exclusively within your character's home community.

### **< STEP 12: COMMUNITY CONTENT BOUNDARIES >**
Because *TSI* is a highly realistic, dark project, themes of intense combat gore, psychological trauma, and mature narratives are completely valid within your independent canon. # **<(SECTION III: THE FIVE HISTORICAL ERAS OF TSI)>**

When writing your character's chronological history, track their status and development through these five explicit eras of Omnian history, fully grounded in the planet's automated macro-systems:

### **< ERA 01: THE PROLOGUE (THE AGE OF RADIANCE) >**
*   **CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE** :: Year 1 to 483,247,185,934,612 O.A. *(Aevum 483,247, Track 185,934,612)*.
*   **THE AUTOMATED MACRO-SYSTEM** :: Time operates as a physical gearset governed by a massive **36-hour rotation** split into 12 hours of high-frequency photon Radiance, 12 hours of atmospheric Twilight, and 12 hours of deep-freeze Shade.
*   **THE INFRASTRUCTURE MONTHS** :: The calendar is divided into 8 Primordial months (34 days each) and 2 Ascendant blocks (35 days each) for planetary maintenance:
	*   *Mensis Ignis* (Fire Furnaces) | *Mensis Aqua* (Hydraulic Gates) | *Mensis Terra* (Tectonic Weaving) | *Mensis Ferri* (Engine Diagnostics)
	*   *Mensis Florae* (Biosphere Growth) | *Mensis Venti* (Wind Corridors) | *Mensis Fulguris* (Power Grid) | *Mensis Glaciei* (Thermal Sinks)
	*   *Mensis Lucis* (Solar Charging) | *Mensis Inanis* (Matrix Anchoring)
*   **THE WEEKLY RHYTHM** :: Labor follows a strict 7-day engine pulse: *Dies Exordii* (Initiation), *Dies Pulsus* (Max Power), *Dies Genesis* (Land-shaping), *Dies Purgationis* (Vent Clearing), *Dies Columnarum* (Stabilizers), *Dies Claritatis* (Harvest Rest), and *Dies Torporis* (Deep-Freeze Stillness).
*   **THE DIVINE HOLIDAY** :: Every year concludes with the 23-day *Spatium Festivum* (*Mensis Natalis*), where the planet's **Weird Core** absorbs 100% of reality's mechanical strain, letting civilian and titan networks shut down for absolute rest.
*   **GLOBAL STATE ENVIRONMENT** :: Planet Omnia sits at its absolute peak. The global realms coexist in stable geopolitical harmony, connected seamlessly by the economic hub of the *Everlasting Radiance Kingdom*. 
*   **ATMOSPHERIC SYSTEM TONE** :: Hopeful, prosperous, and highly structured. Political maneuvering and regional friction exist, but there is zero conceptual understanding of systemic collapse or global extinction.
*   **OC BACKGROUND IMPACT** :: Characters live highly structured, traditional lives within established guilds or orders. They hone their primary or fused elements through disciplined, safe, academic methods. Trauma here is strictly personal, never societal.

### **< ERA 02: THE GREAT CULLING (THE CONVERGENCE OF NULL) >**
*   **CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE** :: Year 483,247,185,934,612 to 483,247,185,934,691 O.A. *(Track 185,934,691)*.
*   **GLOBAL STATE ENVIRONMENT** :: A historic **79-Solar-Track macro-fracture** initiated by a **79-Day planetary blitzkrieg**. **Axian** unleashes a catastrophic invasion, systematically firebombing and tearing apart the *Everlasting Radiance Kingdom*. The automated clockwork systems are forcefully corrupted, causing seasonal core cycles to stall or wildly overlap. Whole bloodlines are broken, and populations are displaced across massive, hostile distances as geography shifts under erratic surges.
*   **ATMOSPHERIC SYSTEM TONE** :: Terrifying, chaotic, urgent, and apocalyptically dark. Defined by sudden, staggering loss, intense combat carnage, and raw desperation.
*   **OC BACKGROUND IMPACT** :: This era is the definitive origin point for profound physical and psychological trauma. Your character's backstory must outline *how* Axian disrupted their life. Elemental control becomes deeply corrupted by trauma—erratic, volatile, and dangerously unstable, triggered unpredictably by pure adrenaline, grief, or panic rather than structured training.

### **< ERA 03: THE DARK AGE (THE RESILIENCE OF SURVIVORS) >**
*   **CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE** :: Year 483,247,185,934,691 to [W.I.P.]
*   **GLOBAL STATE ENVIRONMENT** :: The grand mass-communication lines and automated transit rails are completely severed. Continental communities are isolated into distinct, hyper-vigilant pockets surrounded by corrupted elemental dead zones or rogue warbands. However, the ruined *Everlasting Radiance Kingdom* undergoes a slow, grueling, but miraculous full recovery, standing once more as a beacon of cross-cultural hope. Local survival guilds, scavenger networks, and independent sub-factions form in the shadows.
*   **ATMOSPHERIC SYSTEM TONE** :: Grim, gritty, cautious, yet fiercely resilient. A quiet, tense era of reconstruction, tactical survival, and hit-and-run warfare.
*   **OC BACKGROUND IMPACT** :: Characters operate as hardened, cynical, or traumatized survivors. They must navigate regional ruins using forged equipment and weapons made of scarce elemental materials. Storylines center on intense resource management, navigating ruins, and processing the severe psychological fallout and triggers left behind by Era 2.
