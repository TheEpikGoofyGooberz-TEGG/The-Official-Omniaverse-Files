<(THE LIGHTNING PRINCE SWORDMAN'S NEAR DEATH INCIDENT)>

​The sky above the Stormy Lands was a vast, heavy expanse of shifting grey. Prince Zaki moved through the fields of short, vibrant green grass with the low-slung prowl of a seasoned hunter. Gripped firmly in his hand was his Techno Volt sword, its blade humming with a rhythmic electrical charge.

Surrounding his body was a faint, shimmering aura—a Light Spell Light elementians had casted on him for protection from the Infection.

​In a world where the Titans were infected ten years ago, this barrier was his only shield. For a decade, the massive guardians of Omnia had been transformed into planetary-scale nightmares, and for a decade, the nations had lived in the shadow of their rogue gods.

Zaki’s internal Pulse-Sync was a steady, golden frequency that harmonized with the natural electricity dancing across the Aether-Steel ruins—a survival tactic in a land where the atmosphere itself had turned predatory.

​The air didn't just tingle; it detonated.

​A spray of Blue Sparks tore the atmosphere apart as the Infected Storm-Vanguard V-0 glitched into reality. 6 meters of "buff" Aether-Steel and pulsing, dark-red infected flesh hit the ground with the force of a falling star. 120 tons of momentum turned the soil into a liquid grave, throwing Zaki into the air.

​Blink.

​The V-0 didn't run; it Coordinate-Shifted. It reappeared in a flicker, its massive, muscular hydraulic arm lashing out. The Hook-Claw hissed through the rain—a jagged streak of cold metal that bit deep into Zaki’s shoulder, pinning him and his Techno Volt sword into the mud. From the machine's one Lightning Eye, a swirling vortex of Blue Flame began to surge down the metal chain.

The infection-laden energy lashed against Zaki's Light Spell, hissing and popping as the divine barrier held firm, protecting the Prince's soul from the ten-year-old plague.
​Then, the world simply stopped.

​< THE NANOSECOND OF ERASURE >

​In the heartbeat between the Hook-Claw’s bite and the Blue Flame’s pulse, the watcher arrived. Astrapius collapsed the distance in a single, reality-bending leap.

​The atmosphere turned a bruised, violent red, vibrating with the friction of a god's descent. Astrapius stood tall, his high-tech humanoid frame pulsing with raw voltage. He had been a monster for ten years, his mind long ago replaced by the repeating command: KILL.

At the center of his helm, his lone, singular eye burned with a jagged, corrupted brilliance. Because the Tether-Snap had occurred a decade prior, he was hitting the ground with his full Moon-tier mass, yet his landing was eerily precise—no earthquake, just the terrifying silence of a hunter who has found his prey.

​————————————————————————

​In less than a nanosecond, the Titan performed a feat of total, clinical overkill:

​0.1 Nanoseconds: Astrapius Thunderstepped into the V-0's blind spot. His movement was so surgical that the ground beneath his feet didn't even crack; he simply displaced the space the robot occupied.

​0.5 Nanoseconds: Fueled by a raw, lightning-hot survival instinct, Zaki zipped away. He blurred across the grass, his Techno Volt sword still clutched in his hand, his Light Spell flickering but holding as he escaped the "Zero-Point" just as the Titan’s Dual Blades ignited.

​0.9 Nanoseconds: Astrapius triggered a 1000% Overclock. He became a Localized Quasar, a sun-bright eruption of primordial force. The 120-ton V-0 didn't just break—it was unmade, its atoms shredded and scattered into the void.

​The V-0 was deleted from the coordinates.

< ​THE THREE-MINUTE BLACKOUT & RESTART >

​Zaki didn't see the Titan leave. Even with his Light Spell protecting him from the infection, the atmospheric pressure of an Overclocked strike was a physical wall. The shockwave of the V-0’s erasure slammed into his chest, throwing him across the newly formed glass crater.

​His world went black. His heart stalled, short-circuited by the electrical overflow of a god’s presence. Astrapius stood for a moment in the silence of the red-tinted air, his single eye a jagged rift of corrupted lightning, looking down at the "debris" of the boy. The Titan "blinked" toward the horizon before Roaring, continuing the hunt he had started ten years ago.

​The vacuum trail left by Astrapius’s departure collapsed with the force of a nuclear implosion. That massive kinetic hammer slammed into Zaki’s ribs—a divine, accidental defibrillator.
​Zaki’s eyes snapped open. The golden light in his pupils flickered, then roared to life. He gasped, his lungs burning with the heat of the glass ruin.

He rolled onto his side, his fingers still locked around the hilt of his Techno Volt sword, his Light Spell slowly reforming around him.

​He zipped.
