[THE SHADE'S INCARNATE: AU]

<(THE CONSEQUENCE OF CONTAINING AXIAN'S INFLUENCE)>

​The process of absorbing 23 Flesh-Warped Titans and their 1000% Overclocked souls is not a victory for Alkai—it is a slow-motion execution. While his external mass remains unchanged and the infection never spills into the world, the internal cost of being a Universal Containment Unit manifests as a total collapse of his elemental identity.

​————————————————————————

​1. THE FALTERING: THE SHIELD BREACH 

​Alkai’s Light and Void elements function as the "Dyson Sphere" for his soul. They do not kill the infection; they merely cage it. When his mental or physical stamina hits the breaking point, these two "Ascendant Divines" flicker and fail.

​The Temporal Snap: The transition is instantaneous. There is no fade. One millisecond he is a weary Elementian; the next, he is a glitch in reality.

​The Crimson Silhouette: For the duration of the falter, Alkai’s physical features—his face, his clothes, his very texture—are deleted. He becomes a flat, light-devouring shadow of pure crimson energy.

​The Glitch-Screech: He doesn't scream like a human. Because his Sound/Resonance and Lightning elements are misfiring, his voice becomes a high-frequency, electronic screech—metallic and distorted, echoing with the agony of a thousand corrupted audio files.

​The Pulse: This silhouette does not move naturally. It vibrates at a frequency that mimics the 1.25 Trillion-kill-per-second genocide clock. To look at him is to see the visual representation of every soul lost during the 23-Day Grace.

​————————————————————————

​2. SENSORY RED-OUT (The Drowning)

​From Alkai’s perspective, the "Falter" is a total sensory takeover.

​Chromogenic Blindness: His vision doesn't just turn red; it becomes a viscous, pulsing crimson void. He loses all depth perception and spatial awareness. The world becomes a flat plane of blood-light.

​The Cacophony of 23: Without the Light to filter the frequencies, the pulses of all 23 elements hit his brain simultaneously. 
The 1 GHz Lightning jitter clashes with the Abyssal hollow beat, creating a mental static so loud it effectively paralyzes his nervous system.

​————————————————————————

​3. NEUROLOGICAL PARALYSIS & BERSERKER SHIFT

​The state begins with Internal Gravitational Friction, but quickly escalates into a loss of self as the infection hijacks his nervous system.

​The Ghost-Weight: Even though his external weight is normal, the Void element is no longer "lifting" the spiritual weight of the Titans. Alkai feels the 8,126g of Omnia’s natural gravity pressing directly onto his consciousness.

​Systemic Failure & Aggression: His muscles initially "short-circuit," making movement feel like swimming through liquid lead. However, as the infection floods him, he shifts into a hyper-aggressive state. He lashes out at anything that moves, not to win, but because his existence is a localized explosion of pain he is trying to "erase."

​————————————————————————

​4. THE SNAP-BACK (The Re-Rendering)

​When the Light and Void finally force the infection back into the "cage," the recovery is violent.

​The Containment Pop: The crimson silhouette collapses inward with a sound like a vacuum seal breaking. Alkai "snaps" back into his normal form, often collapsing from the sheer shock of his physical senses returning all at once.

​The Lingering Shadow: After a falter, Alkai remains "cold" to Seismic Vision. For several minutes, he is a Dead Zone—invisible to the planet’s natural rhythms—as his body manually re-syncs with the Omnia-pulse.
​Summary of the State:

​————————————————————————

​"Alkai is no longer a person; he is a map of the genocide, held together by a flickering shield that is destined to break."
