In the 10th year of the Dark Age, the boundary of home was no longer a threshold, but a cliffside. Alkai (17), Zeoxi (24), Vesta (16), and Yuki (15) stepped beyond the wards of their Home, venturing into an Omnia that had been rotting under Axian’s Influence for a decade.

Their goal was discovery; the reality was survival.

The silence of the Earth Lands didn't break—it shattered. A rift tore through the bruised-red sky, bleeding static as Axian’s monsters—masses of pulsing red flesh fused into rusted Aether-Steel—dropped from the coordinates above.

The ambush was surgical. Outnumbered and outmatched by the high-frequency screech of the infection.

< ​THE SEPARATION >

​Alkai, Yuki, Vesta, and Zeoxi departed from their home to explore Omnia. The four were ambushed after entering the Earth Lands by Axian’s Ambushers, dropped from a rift that tore open in the sky above.

​The ambush separated Alkai from the others. Trying to regroup with Axian’s goons in the way meant certain death, so Alkai took a different path. While the others fled to the Ice Kingdom, Alkai headed for the Lightning Kingdom—the only place nearby to seek safety. But with no idea where the others ended up, it could take weeks or even a month or more to find them again.

​Suddenly, a whoosh of wind zips beside Alkai. Alkai gasp, freezing in his tracks. Alkai turned to see what went past him: a Thunderbolt Arrow now embedded in the mud.

Alkai spotted a black silhouette standing on top of a huge rock, yellow-greenish predatory eyes illuminating in the darkness their hood created over their head.. They were holding a bow, an arrow already nocked, partially drawn and aimed right at Alkai's chest. Lightning flashes behind the figure, showing who it is...

Somewhere far beyond the horizon, the Infected Astrapius had just finished a lethal engagement, and the shockwave of its victory-cry traveled through the air like a physical weight.
