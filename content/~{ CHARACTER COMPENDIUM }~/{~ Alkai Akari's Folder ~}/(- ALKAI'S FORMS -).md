### **< 1. THE SHADE FORM (THE SAVAGE DARK FORCE) >**

* **FOCUS:** IRRESISTIBLE FORCE & SPATIOTEMPORAL LOCKDOWN.
* **THEME:** THE LIVING SINGULARITY.

* ***ADVANTAGES:***
    * **VARIABLE DENSITY:** Alkai can adjust his weight mid-swing—from 0 mass (maximum speed) to mountain-level mass (infinite momentum) at the moment of impact.
    * **GRAVITATIONAL TETHERING:** Dynamic control over the Event Horizon to pull enemies or liquefy targets.
    * **SHADOW-JITSU (ADVANCED):** Precision control over probability collapse; can stack or spread strike density.
    * **ABSURD DURABILITY & STRENGTH:** Functions as an immovable object capable of tanking Titan-scale impacts and exerting force that bypasses conventional structural integrity.
    
* ***WEAKNESSES:***
    * **ENVIRONMENTAL HOSTILITY:** Potential to sink into the planet's core if mass exceeds structural limits.
    * **MOMENTUM OVERSHOOT:** Rigid trajectory due to extreme mass/speed.
    * **PSYCHOLOGICAL TUNNEL VISION:** Risks losing tactical composure to the "crushing" aggression of the form.

### **< 2. THE RADIANT FORM (THE HOLY GLASS CANNON) >**

* **FOCUS:** FLAWLESS PRECISION & RELATIVISTIC MOBILITY.
* **THEME:** THE ARCHITECT OF LIGHT.

* ***ADVANTAGES:***
    * **MATTER-PHASING:** By adjusting light-frequency, Alkai can phase through solid matter, rendering him immune to physical contact when active.
    * **RELATIVISTIC DODGE-TANKING:** Operates entirely on evasion; moves at light-speed frequencies to ensure no physical attack can lock onto his signature.
    * **ADJUSTABLE REFRACTION:** Non-linear movement; curves trajectories in spirals or arcs, making him mathematically impossible to predict.
    * **HARD-LIGHT RANGED ARTISTRY:** Purely ranged output; utilizes "Solidified Holy Bolts" for surgical assassination or wide-area bombardment.

* ***WEAKNESSES:***
    * **EXTREME FRAGILITY:** The form sheds all physical mass to achieve light-speed mobility; a single direct hit that bypasses evasion/phasing will shatter his structural integrity.
    * **ENERGY SIGNATURE:** High visibility; acts as a beacon for all hostile sensors.
    * **SENSORY OVERLOAD:** High cognitive cost to filter the movement of every atom in the vicinity.


#### **< 2.1 RADIANT FLIGHT: PHOTONIC DRIFT >**

​**MECHANIC:** Unlike conventional flight, Alkai does not rely on aerodynamic lift. By shedding physical mass to enter a "Photonic State," he traverses the environment by "surfing" the wavelengths of ambient light.

* ***ADVANTAGES:**
    * ​**NULL-RESISTANCE TRAVERSAL:** Because he exists as a frequency of light during movement, he is entirely immune to wind resistance, atmospheric pressure, and drag, allowing for instantaneous acceleration to terminal velocity.
    * **REFRACTIVE VECTORS:** By manipulating his internal light-signature, he can "bend" his flight path in non-Euclidean curves. This allows for abrupt, impossible direction changes mid-air—such as hard 90-degree turns—without losing momentum.
    * **PHOTON-STEPPING:** Alkai can briefly solidify points of light beneath him, creating momentary, invisible platforms in mid-air. This allows him to "climb" space itself or hover in complete stasis even without atmospheric support.

* ***LIMITATIONS:***
    * **LUMINAL DEPENDENCY:** In areas of absolute darkness (or artificial null-light zones), his flight velocity is significantly throttled, as there are no external light-wavelengths to anchor or "drift" upon, forcing him to rely on his own energy expenditure to propel himself. Luckily he's a Blinding Light Source and surf the wavelengths of the Light he illuminates.

### **< 2.2 THE "SLIDER" SYSTEM >**

* **MECHANIC:** Alkai’s ability to balance between forms to create hybrid combat styles.

* **ADJUSTMENT LEVELS:**
    * **80% SHADE / 20% RADIANT (THE METEOR):** Moves at Mach speeds but retains enough mass to level a city block upon impact.
    * **50% SHADE / 50% RADIANT (THE VOID-STAR):** A balanced state where he can phase through attacks and counter-attack with crushing gravity.
    * **20% SHADE / 80% RADIANT (THE NEEDLE):** Moves at near-light speed with just enough mass to ensure his "Hard Light" bolts have physical "kick" and knockback.
    
* **TACTICAL PIVOT:** Pivoting to Radiant Form acts as an emergency evasion state; shedding density allows him to phase through "kill-shots" that would otherwise destroy Shade Form.

* **ULTIMATE WEAKNESS (ADJUSTMENT LAG):** The split-second transition where Alkai is neither heavy nor fast, causing massive energy release (shockwaves/flashes).

### **< THE ARCHITECT'S BURDEN (POCKET DIMENSIONS) >**

Alkai is capable of making a **Pocket-Dimesion** with his Forms, each one mirroring what ***Form*** he's using. He can forcefully teleport a Target of any kind (E.g., Anomalies/Axian's Infections *or* a Deity) into one of these Pocket Dimesions that his active form constructed. Though using these Pocket dimensions can have its *burdens*.

* **MENTAL STRAIN:** Holding up the dimension is physically and mentally exhausting; every strike inside reverberates through his nervous system.
* **ENERGY LEAK:** Complex physics (like Dim Form’s Causal Dominance) cause rapid energy drain.
* **FEEDBACK LOOP:** Damage to the environment inside the dimension is damage to Alkai.
* **COLLAPSE PENALTY:** Premature collapse causes spatial implosions, ejection into the physical world, and potential total vulnerability.
* **STRATEGIC USAGE:** Used against Titans (due to their size/destructiveness) and Axian (a terrifying gamble to end the fight before he consumes the arena walls).
