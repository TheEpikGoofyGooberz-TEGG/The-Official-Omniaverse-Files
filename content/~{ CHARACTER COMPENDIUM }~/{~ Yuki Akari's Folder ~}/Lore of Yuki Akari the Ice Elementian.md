​The silence of the Earth Lands didn't break—it shattered. A rift tore through the bruised-red sky, bleeding static as Axian’s monsters—masses of pulsing red flesh fused into rusted Aether-Steel—dropped from the coordinates above.

​The ambush was surgical. Outnumbered and outmatched by the high-frequency screech of the infection, the group’s coordination was forcibly disrupted.

​< THE SEPARATION >

​The coordinate-grid of their journey collapsed. Forced into a desperate Zip-retreat, the four were scattered like shrapnel. To stay together was to die together.

​< THE SNOWY LANDS >

​The trio breached the borders of the Snowy Lands. Here, the vibrant green of the world died, replaced by a jagged, crystalline silence. The air grew thin, cold enough to freeze the very breath in their lungs.

​But in the Dark Age, the cold is a tactical mask.
​While the Infected Titans prowl the clouds, the Elemental Deities have not fallen—they have vanished into deep hiding, remaining pure and uncorrupted.

Yuki looked toward the frost-covered peaks of the Ice Kingdom, hoping the sub-zero temperatures would hide their presence from the monsters hunting them. The escape was over, but the survival in the frost had only just begun.
