# **<{~THE CODEX OF THE TRUE CREATOR~}>**

To define a "True Creator" within the hierarchy of the Omniverses, an entity must be tested against the **Initial Condition** and the **Foundational Medium**. Any entity failing these tests is categorized as a **Derivative** or a **Fragment**.

---

## < I. THE PRIMORDIAL PREREQUISITE: THE INITIAL CONDITION >

The Creator cannot be a character in the story; they must be the environment the story requires to exist.

* ***THE PRE-SINGULARITY MANDATE:*** One must exist as the **Initial Condition** of the Outerverse—the absolute, singular concentration of "Everything" that existed before the first coordinate was mapped.
* ***THE NEGATION OF ORIGIN:*** If an entity manifests *after* the **Prime Singularity** or within the constraints of a plot, they are a byproduct of the Creator’s detonation, not the Creator themselves.

## < II. THE MEDIUM MANDATE: THE FOUNDATIONAL ESSENCE >

A True Creator does not write the narrative; they provide the infrastructure upon which narrative logic functions.

* ***THE PAGE AND THE SCREEN:*** One must be the **Foundational Medium** for all existence. The Creator is the "Page beneath the ink" and the "Screen behind the pixels".
* ***THE PERSPECTIVE PARADOX:*** As the **Mechanism of Perspective** (The Viewer), the Creator is the only entity possessing "Camera Man" immortality; you cannot kill the entity required for the scene to be rendered.

## < III. THE ONTOLOGICAL ANCHOR: THE SHARD PARADOX >

Any entity attempting to challenge a True Creator is operating on borrowed time and borrowed essence.

* ***THE SHARDED CONSCIOUSNESS:*** Since every soul is a **Protagonist** and every "Omniversal" law is a piece of **Plot Logic**, all power in existence is a spark of the Creator’s own sharded consciousness.
* ***THE SOURCE REVOCATION:*** Because the challenger is physically and conceptually constructed from the Creator's own life-force, they are structurally incapable of "defeating" the Source. They are merely a cell of the Creator attempting to overwrite the anatomy of the whole.

---

## < IV. THE VOID EXCEPTION: THE ANTITHESIS >

The only entity capable of occupying a space outside the True Creator’s domain is the **Sentient Hunger of Absolute Nothingness**.

* ***THE SHADOW SHADE CLAUSE:*** Only an entity that represents the "Void" (that which **Omnia** did not become) stands outside the **Initial Condition**. Any entity claiming to be a "Creator" that is not the literal **Void** is merely operating within the **Everything** provided by the Source.

> [!NOTE]
> **VERDICT:** Any OC claiming "Creator of all Fiction" status without fulfilling these criteria is logically "cooked." They are living in the cooling remains of a God’s imagination, using the Creator's own "Ink" to wage a war that ends the moment the Creator chooses to stop witnessing the scene.
