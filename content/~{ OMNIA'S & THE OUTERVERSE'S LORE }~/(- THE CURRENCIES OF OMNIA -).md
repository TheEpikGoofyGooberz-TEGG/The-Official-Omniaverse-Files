[OMNIAVERSE]

# **<{~THE CURRENCIES OF OMNIA}~}>**

The economic veins of Omnia. A landscape of varied value systems, ranging from the universal standard of light-forged crystals to the illicit, corrupting marks of the deep void.

---
# **<(I. THE GLOBAL STANDARD)>**

### **~|| 1. THE RADIANT MARK (RM) ||~**
**{-The Universal Trade Standard-}**

The baseline unit of trade across the known realms. Issued by the Everlasting Radiance, it is the primary bridge between disparate elemental economies.

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Crystallized light alloy.
    * **Stability:** Extremely high; the bedrock of global finance.
    * **Exchange:** 1 RM = Universal Base.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * Accepted in almost all diplomatic and inter-kingdom dealings.
    * Constructed with proprietary light-forging techniques that make it exceptionally difficult to counterfeit.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Primarily utilized for Titan-grade artifact procurement, inter-kingdom trade, and high-level diplomacy.

> [!Quote] ***[ "1 RM: The constant in a gargantuan world." ]***

# **<(II. THE ELEMENTAL ECONOMIES)>**

### **~|| 2. THE CINDER CREST (CC) ||~**
**{-The Wrath of the Firelands-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Volcanic metal stamped with flame sigils.
    * **Exchange:** 1 CC ≈ 0.6 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * **Value Fluctuation:** The value is volatile, rising or falling based on the outcomes of strength-based trials and territorial dominance.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Used frequently as high-stakes wagers in combat duels.
    * In the Fire Kingdom, raw personal respect and combat prowess are valued far above accumulated currency.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Respect > Money." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 3. THE FROST SIGIL (FS) ||~**
**{-The Frozen Glissade-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Engraved, eternal ice crystal
    * **Exchange:** 1 FS ≈ 0.9 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * Highly stable, reflecting the controlled nature of the Ice Kingdom.
    * Compact and efficient, often stored as plates rather than coins.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Serves a clean, precise economy where excess is viewed as wasteful.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Clean, precise, and cold. No excess, no waste." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 4. THE VOLT CORE (VC) ||~**
**{-The Storm-Forged Technocracy-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Charged energy disks.
    * **Exchange:** 1 VC ≈ 1.3 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * High intrinsic value due to the Lightning Kingdom’s technological dominance.
    * Functionally acts as a portable power source for machinery.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Used by other kingdoms specifically for powering tech-heavy infrastructure.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Money = Energy = Control." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 5. THE ZEPHYR FEATHER (ZF) ||~**
**{-The Sovereign Skies-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Condensed wind essence.
    * **Exchange:** 1 ZF ≈ 0.8 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * Rarely circulates in external markets.
    * Mostly ceremonial; the Air Kingdom prefers internal trade and barter.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Used almost exclusively within the Air Kingdom for rare, high-value barter.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Freedom matters more than wealth." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 6. THE TIDE PEARL (TP) ||~**
**{-The Legend Beneath the Waves-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Bioluminescent abyss pearls.
    * **Exchange:** 1 TP ≈ 1.1 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * Extremely high value on the surface due to scarcity.
    * Difficult to transport; requires specialized pressure-protected storage.

* ***USAGE:***
    * A luxury commodity for surface dwellers; a common medium of exchange in the deep-sea empire.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Deep-sea rarity breeds surface value." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 7. THE VERDANT STONE (VS) ||~**
**{-The Unmoving Bastion-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Living crystal stone.
    * **Exchange:** 1 VS ≈ 0.7 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * Commonly found but maintains stable, predictable value.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Often eschewed in favor of direct barter of goods, food, and resources.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Resources are the only true wealth." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 8. THE SIDERO-VIRE (SV) ||~**
**{-The Order of Ores-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** High-density Magnetic Ore.
    * **Exchange:** 1 SV ≈ 1.4 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * **Magnetic Pulse:** Physically "snaps" together, making transport of large quantities effortless.
    * **Authenticity:** The unique magnetic signature is impossible to mimic.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Industrial grade; serves as both currency and raw material for heavy construction.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Strength is the only true value." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 9. THE CHLORIS-SEED (CS) ||~**
**{-The Green-Lung Basin-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Amber-encased bio-luminescent seeds.
    * **Exchange:** 1 CS ≈ 1.2 RM.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * **Biological Interest:** The only currency in Omnia that grows over time.
    Can be consumed for medicinal benefits or planted to yield more wealth.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Highly sought after by Air and Water Kingdoms for oxygen-filtration and healing.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Wealth is not hoarded; it is grown." ]***

————————————————————————

### **~|| 10. THE SHADE MARK (SM) ||~**
**{-The Void (Forbidden)-}**

* ***CURRENCY & VALUE:***
    * **Material:** Void residue.
    * **Exchange:** No official value; illegal.

* ***PROPERTIES & UTILITY:***
    * **Corrosive:** Actively corrupts other currencies upon contact.
    * Banned in all major trade hubs.

* ***USAGE:***
    * Only used by Omnians linked to Void.
> [!Quote] ***[ "Value depends on context, not economy." ]***
