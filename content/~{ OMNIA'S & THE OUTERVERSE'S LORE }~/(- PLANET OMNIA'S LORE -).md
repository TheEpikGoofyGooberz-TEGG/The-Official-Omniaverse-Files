# **<{~PLANET OMNIA~}>**


Planet Omnia is a gargantuan inhabitable planet. Being 8,126× bigger than Earth. It is a primordial entity, existing since the Big bang happened. It serves as the cosmic anchor for the elemental constants.

*   ***THE GLOBAL SCALE:*** While original Earth is a mere 510 million km², Omnia covers a staggering 33.68 Quadrillion km² ($33,682,752,000,000,000\text{ km}^2$) and is home to a massive population of over 6.7 Quintillion ($6,736,550,400,000,000,000$) inhabitants.

*   ***THE RESTRAINED CORE:*** Having existed since the dawn of all reality, it is an ancient, weathered world. Its immense gravity is tempered by a condensed , weird but magical core, allowing its colossal civilization to flourish without being crushed under its own weight, aided constantly by the Elemental Titans to prevent planetary stress.

*   ***OMNIA'S SIZE VS. EARTH'S SUN:*** Omnia is 74 times wider than the Sun and has the mass of 1.6 million Suns.

# **<(THE SPECIES: ELEMENTIANS)>**

The native inhabitants of Omnia, defined by their biological connection to the planet's baseline energy.

*   ***THE OMNIA-PULSE:*** Every Elementian is born with a secondary, luminous circulatory system that pulses with raw, visible elemental energy.

*   ***PURE-BLOODS:*** Individuals who possess and manipulate a single, unadulterated element (e.g., Fire, Water, Earth).

*   ***HYBRIDS:*** Individuals born with two or more distinct elements that coexist side-by-side within their system without mixing.

*   ***FUSED ELEMENTIANS (Synthesized Wholes):*** A rare and exceptionally powerful state where two or more base elements permanently bond on a molecular level, transforming into a completely New Whole Element. For example, Fire + Earth does not merely remain two separate elements; it synthesizes into Magma. This new element possesses its own unique properties, strengths, and weaknesses that neither original base element possessed alone.

# **<(ELEMENTAL TECHNOLOGY SCALE)>**

*   ***BRIDGING THE INFINITE:*** On a planet with a circumference exceeding 325,311,920 km, standard transit is entirely useless. Advanced elemental technology is the only mechanism capable of bridging the otherwise impossible geographical distances between nations.

# **<(THE ELEMENTAL TITANS)>**

Colossal guardians (dozens of kilometers tall) acting as the physical manifestations of their Deities' elements.

*   ***THE ANCHORS:*** The Titans serve as the literal physical stabilizers for Omnia’s 536 billion-fold mass. Without their constant intervention, the sheer gravitational pull of a world 1.6 million times more massive than the Sun would cause it to ignite into a Hypermassive Quasi-Star or a Direct-Collapse Black Hole—phenomena far more terrifying than a standard massive star.

*   ***TECHNICAL CHECK:*** A Blue Supergiant is a specific stage of a very high-mass star. Given Omnia's mass of 536 billion Earths (roughly 1.6 million times the mass of the Sun), it wouldn't just be a star; it would be one of the most massive stars ever conceived, instantly hitting the Eddington Limit where its own intense light would blow its outer layers apart.

*   ***GRAVITY:*** At this scale, Omnia's natural baseline gravity is an absolute 8,126g.

*   ***THE TITANS:*** By actively negating 99.987% of this crushing natural gravity, the Titans maintain a stable, habitable 1g environment across the surface.

*   ***THE SENTINELS:*** They functioned historically as the primary protectors of their respective nations, shielding civilizations from catastrophic environmental disasters and maintaining the balance of their lands.

*   ***THE POWER OF THE ANCHOR:*** To negate 99.987% of 8,126g down to a comfortable 1g, the Titans perform a feat of constant energy output that dwarfs the cumulative production of a thousand galaxies.

These beings do not merely stand upon the crust; they function as Gravitational Heat Sinks. As physical manifestations of their Deities, they act as cosmic conduits, piping the excess gravitational potential energy of Omnia out into the Omniverse to prevent the planet from collapsing or igniting.

When subjected to the 1000% Overclock, a Titan—already capable of stabilizing a 536-billion-Earth-mass world—effectively becomes a walking "Localized Quasar," a localized eruption of primordial force.

# **<(THE GRAVITATIONAL UMBILICAL: THE CORE-LOCK PROTOCOL)>**

To exist on a world with a natural gravity of 8,126g, the Titans cannot rely on physical structural integrity alone. Their presence is a feat of Active Spatial Anchoring.

### **< 1. THE MECHANIC: Sub-Spatial Redirection >**

Titans do not simply "stand" on the planetary crust of Omnia. Instead, they project an invisible, highly concentrated beam of gravitational force—the Umbilical—that extends directly from their center of mass, pierces through the crust, and locks into the Condensed Core of the planet.

*   ***PRESSURE DISPLACEMENT:*** 100% of the Titan’s moon-tier mass is diverted down through this spatial tether.

*   ***THE "ZERO-G" FOOTPRINT:*** Because their immense weight is being "pulled" toward the core through a sub-spatial channel rather than pushing "down" on the immediate dirt, a Titan standing directly on a flower wouldn't even crush its petals.

*   ***THE STABILIZER:*** This tether acts as a two-way street. It allows the Titans to siphon the excess gravitational energy of Omnia and vent it safely into the Omniverse, preventing the planet from collapsing into a Black Hole.

​————————————————————————
### **< 2. THE AXIAN CORRUPTION >**

Under the 1000% Overclock, Axian has weaponized this safety feature by forcing the infected Titans to reverse the polarity of their Umbilicals.

*   ***THE OVERLOAD:*** Instead of venting excess energy away from the core, the Titans are now violently sucking primordial energy out of the core to power their 10x Ascension.

*   ***THE DISASTER:*** 25% of the Sub-Spatial Tether is gone. This loss is the direct cause of the catastrophic Magnitude-7.3 earthquakes trailing every step. The crust being crushed by the Titans' moon-tier mass for the very first time.
