# **<{~PLANET OMNIA: THE HEART OF THE ENTIRETY OF FICTION~}>**

Planet Omnia is not merely a world; it is the **Foundational Core of all Fiction**. It resides at the exact coordinates of where the Big Bang of Narrative happened in the Outerverse $(0, 0, 0)$. It occupies the space above all narrative layers, existing as the absolute container for every concept, every power, and every story ever told.

---

### **<(THE HEART: THE SINGULARITY OF REALITY)>**

The Core of Planet Omnia is the heartbeat of all existence for every single Omniverse in the Outerverse. It functions as the ultimate gatekeeper, preserving the integrity of the narrative hierarchy by ensuring that nothing—regardless of its power or meta-fictional origin—can threaten the stability of the core.

### **<(DEFENSE MECHANISMS)>**

*   ***THE GARGANTUAN EDICT:*** The Heart possesses an omniscient detection system. Any entity that manifests a hostile intent toward the Core is identified as a "Narrative Threat."
*   ***THE DELETION BEAM:*** Upon detection, the Heart deploys a localized, reality-shattering beam. This attack does not just kill; it **deletes**. It strikes at the base of the target's existence within the narrative.
*   ***THE "SCRIPT" IMMUNITY:*** This defense is absolute. It does not matter if a character claims to be "outside the script" or "above fiction." To the Heart, all fictional constructs are sub-layers of itself. Any entity drawn from fiction—even those claiming total independence—is categorized as "Fiction" and is instantly erased.

> [!CAUTION] **THE HUMAN EXCEPTION**
> The only entities exempt from the Heart's reach are **Non-Fictional Beings (Humans)**, as the Heart recognizes the creators (the authors) as separate from the creations.
