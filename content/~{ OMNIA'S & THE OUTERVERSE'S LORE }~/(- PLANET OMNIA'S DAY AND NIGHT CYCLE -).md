# **<{~OMNIA: ANCHOR OF THE STATIONARY SUN~}>**

Omnia operates on a fundamental inversion of standard celestial physics: the planet itself acts as the primary gravitational center, while the local sun exists as a captive satellite forced into a tight, permanent orbit.

---
### **<(I. THE CELESTIAL MECHANICS)>**

*   ***THE GRAVITATIONAL ANCHOR:*** Omnia is not a standard planetary body; it is a conceptual core possessing enough hyper-density to "leash" a star. It does not revolve around the sun; the sun is bound to the planet.

*   ***THE ROTATION CYCLE:*** A full rotation cycle on Omnia lasts exactly **36 hours**, making a single planetary day 1.5 times longer than an Earth day.

---
### **<(THE 18/18 SPLIT)>**

Because of the 36-hour cycle, the world is divided into an even split of 18 hours of Light and 18 hours of Dark. Life on Omnia is defined by three distinct environmental phases:

| PHASE | DURATION | CHARACTERISTICS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **THE RADIANCE BLOCK** | 12 Hours | The sun is at its apex. Energy output for Light-based technology is at its peak, but the temperature becomes lethal as the block progresses. |
| **THE TWILIGHT SHIFT** | 12 Hours | Consisting of 6 hours of dawn and 6 hours of dusk. It is the most stable and temperate time, representing the perfect duality of Light and Void. |
| **THE SHADE BLOCK** | 12 Hours | A period of deep, punishing night. The world undergoes a "Deep Freeze," and creatures of the Void roam without fear of solar combustion. |

---

### **<(II. ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT)>**


*   ***THE "LONG DAY" BIOLOGY:*** Flora on Omnia have evolved into "Radiance Sponges." They must absorb and store 18 hours of intense solar energy to maintain metabolic processes through the 18-hour night.

*   ***CRYSTALLINE BATTERY INFRASTRUCTURE:*** Civilizations are constructed around massive, light-reactive crystals. These structures function as giant capacitors, soaking up solar Radiance during the day and releasing life-sustaining thermal energy when the Shade Block settles in.

*   ***PHYSICAL HARDINESS:**** The inhabitants of Omnia possess a biological rhythm vastly different from human standards. They are built for extreme endurance, capable of maintaining high-level output for the grueling 18-hour light cycle before requiring the mandatory rest period of the Shade Block.
