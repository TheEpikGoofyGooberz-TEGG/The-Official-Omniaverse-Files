# **<{~THE KINGDOMS OF OMNIA~}>**

The foundational realms of Omnia, built upon the elements of the Deities and shaped by their geography, culture, and martial philosophies.

# **<(I. THE ASCENDANT DIVINES | Total: 2)>**


### **~|| 1. THE EVERLASTING RADIANCE ||~**
**{-The Bastion of Light and Harmony-}**

The largest public Kingdom in all of Omnia, constructed as a global haven for all kinds of Elementians. This grand realm stands as the universal symbol of planetary peace and unity, offering refuge to the displaced and ensuring an idealized, secure life where hardship rarely breaches its borders.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Sprawling, pristine architecture designed to accommodate diverse elemental needs.
    *   Highly organized, stable infrastructure optimized for universal safety and prosperity.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Calm, composed, and unfailingly respectful.
    *   Highly cooperative across all elemental races; disputes are mediated and resolved internally before escalating.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***CONTROL & PRECISION:*** Light Elementians are trained to end conflicts with supreme discipline and restraint. Fights are concluded instantly rather than brutally.
    *   ***ELITE MARSHAL QUALITY:*** Boasts the most skilled, tactically brilliant, and powerful frontline warriors in Omnia, complemented by the world's finest healers.

*   ***REALM VULNERABILITIES:***
    *   ***PACIFIST NAIVETY:*** A deep-seated overconfidence born from centuries of uninterrupted peace leaves the populace dangerously naive toward covert, hidden threats.
    *   ***COMBAT ATROPHY:*** Due to the kingdom's safety, a significant portion of the civilian population lacks any real battlefield experience.
    *   ***STRICT REGULATION:*** While immensely powerful, Light Elementians operate under heavy bureaucratic and societal restrictions.

> [!Quote] ***[ "The safest place in Omnia for everyone, where every kind of Elementian is welcomed." ]***

### **~|| 2. THE ANCIENT VOID KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Kingdom Beneath Existence-}**

The true largest Kingdom on the planet, established by the primordial Void Elementians. Its staggering architecture is hidden entirely from surface view, buried somewhere deep within the sub-spatial abysses under the planetary crust of Omnia.

 *   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Ancient, monolithic structures and reality-defying relics dating back to the dawn of time.
    *   An isolated subterranean realm completely severed from the global layout.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Silent, profoundly detached, and endlessly observant.
    *   Minimal outward emotion, but driven by absolute, unswerving intent.
    *   Total social isolation; they value the accumulation of cosmic knowledge far above territorial domination.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***EXISTENTIAL WARFARE:*** They view existence itself as a perpetual state of conflict. 
    *   ***UNIVERSAL COUNTER:*** Their martial arts and unique void essence allow them to systematically shut down and counter almost any other element.

*   ***REALM VULNERABILITIES:***
    *   ***POPULATION DECAY:*** Suffers from exceptionally slow reproduction cycles and a dangerously low population density.
    *   ***VOID ATTRITION:*** Long-term exposure to the raw Void exerts a heavy, continuous psychological strain on its citizens.
    *   ***TOTAL ISOLATION:*** Fiercely hostile to all external Elementians, viewing surface life as an anomaly.

> [!Quote] ***[ "You don’t find the Void Kingdom. It finds you." ]***

### **~|| 3. THE FIRE KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Wrath of the Firelands-}**

Situated deep within the volatile, scorched territories known as the Firelands. This is a brutal realm where the environment itself serves as a constant test of survival, demanding absolute strength from anyone who dares walk its borders.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   A landscape dominated by massive volcano chains, rushing rivers of molten slag, and ash-choked skies.
    *   Cities are aggressively forged out of blackened volcanic stone, heat-treated ores, and active living flame conduits.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Fierce, confrontational, and brutally honest.
    *   Quick to anger, exceptionally slow to forgive, and emotionally hardened from birth.
*   ***STRENGTH-BASED HIERARCHY:***
    *   Governance is entirely dictated by raw power. Warlords and generals hold ultimate legislative authority; the weak are marginalized.
*   ***COMBAT LAW:***
    *   Laws are tested and enforced through public combat trials rather than legal courts. Weakness is viewed as an outright sin.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***OVERWHELMING FORCE:*** Focuses on crushing an opponent's defense with unmitigated physical power and enduring immense pain without hesitation.
    *   ***SPARTAN TRAINING:*** Children are entered into deadly training regimens at a young age. Failure is never corrected—it is severely punished.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Immense, explosive raw destructive power.
    *   Complete immunity to extreme thermal environments and high heat.
    *   Unrivaled survival instincts and fearless frontline shock-troopers.

> [!Quote] ***[ "To burn is to live. To fade is to fail. Mercy is rare. Compassion is weakness." ]***

### **~|| 4. THE WATER KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Legend Beneath the Waves-}**

An isolationist aquatic empire situated at the absolute bottom of Omnia's deepest oceans. It rests under crushing abyssal pressure and eternal darkness, leaving its inner workings a complete mystery to the surface world.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Colossal coral megastructures, towering kelp castles, and trench-cities.
    *   Illuminated entirely by complex networks of natural bioluminescent pathways.
    *   Protected by massive, naturally occurring pressure barriers generated by targeted water currents.

*  *** GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Calm, patient, and emotionally fluid; they mirror the changing states of water.
    *   Deeply community-oriented with an emphasis on profound family bonds.
    *   Perceptive and deliberate; they refuse to rush vital decisions, choosing to wait for the correct tide.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***KINETIC REDIRECTION:*** Warriors are trained to never oppose oncoming force directly. They fluidly absorb and redirect attacks, allowing enemies to exhaust themselves against the ocean's weight.
    *   ***PRESSURE TRAINING:*** Focuses heavily on lung capacity, meticulous breath control, and sensory management in total darkness.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Supreme, unmatched mobility and speed while submerged.
    *   Near-infinite stamina and specialized internal resistance to high pressure and pitch-black environments.
    *   Flawless team-wide coordination and non-verbal communication via water currents and swimming patterns.

> [!Quote] ***[ "The ocean never rushes, yet it shapes the world." ]***

### **~|| 5. THE EARTH KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Unmoving Bastion-}**

Spreading across vast, vibrant green plains, rolling hills, and titanic mountain ranges. The Earth Elementians are widely renowned as the most genuinely friendly, hospitable, and peaceful race on Omnia, acting as the planet's ultimate defensive anchors.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Vast, fertile farmlands, dense pristine forests, and subterranean crystal caverns.
    *   Heavily fortified stone cities designed to integrate seamlessly with the natural terrain.
    *   Wild Elemals and Elera roam freely, treated as respected companions rather than tools.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Immensely community-focused, fiercely loyal, patient, and slow to anger.
    *   Guided by elder councils with no rigid social hierarchy; leadership is earned strictly through genuine service and protection of the land.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   **Endure and Conclude:** They train only when absolutely necessary. Their strategy is to absorb the enemy's maximum output first, protect the innocent, and strike only to decisively end a battle. They never chase conflict, but they always finish it.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Immense physical lifting strength and near-infinite cellular endurance.
    *   Natural stone-like armor plating and rapid earth-assisted regeneration.
    *   ***CRITICAL FLAW:*** Their inherent kindness and gentle nature can be easily exploited by highly manipulative factions.

> [!Quote] ***[ "If the land stands, so do we. Peace isn’t weakness, it’s a choice." ]***

​————————————————————————

### **~|| 6. THE AIR KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Sovereign Skies-}**

An ethereal, elusive civilization existing entirely in the upper atmosphere of Omnia. Due to its extreme altitude, its exact coordinates remain almost entirely unknown to ground dwellers, causing most global cartographers to leave its location blank.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Floating cloud-islands and wind-anchored landmasses connected by shifting, invisible air bridges.
    *   Accessible exclusively to Air Elementians or rare outsiders who can read and navigate localized jet streams.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Free-spirited, intensely curious, relaxed, and deeply light-hearted.
    *   Emotionally detached from terrestrial politics; they prefer to quietly observe the chaotic world below rather than participate in shaping it.
    *   Vehemently reject all forms of rigid societal discipline or structural constraints.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***EVASIVE NON-CONFRONTATION:*** Their primary doctrine dictates avoiding direct impact entirely. They escape threats before contact is initiated, defining victory not by defeating the enemy, but by never being struck.
    *   ***WIND-DANCING:*** Traditional sparring is replaced by high-speed aerial races, wind-dancing drills, and kinetic reflex games.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Unmatched aerial mobility, flight control, and supreme three-dimensional awareness.
    *   Mastery over absolute stealth, atmospheric redirection, and evasion.
    *   Virtually impossible to track, corner, trap, or lock down in combat.

> [!Quote] ***[ "The strongest force is the one you never touch. Combat is survival—not domination." ]***

### **~|| 7. THE ICE KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Frozen Glissade-}**

A highly disciplined, elegant kingdom nestled deep within the permanently frozen, snow-swept polar regions of Omnia. Its citizens are highly regarded as the most graceful, well-mannered, and articulate individuals on the planet.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Exquisite ice-crystal architecture that dynamically reflects and channels moonlight.
    *   Interconnected networks of frozen pathways and grand skating bridges that serve as the kingdom's primary transit system.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Polite, kind, fair-minded, and exceptionally well-mannered.
    *   Possess flawless emotional regulation; public displays of uncontrolled emotion or anger are heavily stigmatized as childish and immature.
    *   **Skating Culture:** Skating is integrated into every aspect of life. Children learn to glide smoothly across ice before learning basic combat tricks. It is used equally for travel, artistic historical storytelling, and military deployment.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   **The Death of Dance:** Ice warriors move across the battlefield like performance skaters—smooth, highly efficient, perfectly balanced, and utterly deadly when a strike is required.
    *   ***Sub-zero Meditation:*** Training requires hours of silent meditation in extreme subzero temperatures to maintain absolute psychological calm.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Flawless physical agility, reaction control, and micro-balance.
    *   Impeccable squad coordination and precision strike capabilities.
    *   Inherent, absolute resistance to freezing temperatures and biting polar pressure. 

> [!Quote] ***[ "Grace in movement, frost in intent." ]***

### **~|| 8. THE LIGHTNING KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Storm-Forged Technocracy-}**

A highly advanced, isolationist kingdom located inside Omnia's most volatile storm sectors. The Lightning Elementians are silent, deeply introverted, and hyper-efficient warriors who have weaponized their element to develop the most advanced technology on the planet.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   A landscape of perpetual lightning strikes, towering thunderclouds, and electrified terrain.
    *   Cities are composed of towering conductive metal spires and floating platforms held stable by high-output electromagnetic fields.
    *   High-speed travel is facilitated through a nationwide network of magnetic storm rails.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Extremely silent, introverted, and emotionally distant. 
    *   Cold-hearted and clinical, though not inherently cruel; they filter life through pure logic and absolute efficiency.
    *   They speak only when necessary, treating every spoken word as a calculated resource. Society is governed by rigid, hyper-structured schedules.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***NEURAL OVERLOAD:*** Training focuses heavily on mental acceleration and tactical simulations before physical application. 
    *   ***INSTANT TERMINATION:*** Strategy revolves around ending battles in a fraction of a second. They do not engage in prolonged fights, favoring high-precision vital strikes over raw physical power.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   ***TECHNOLOGICAL SUPERIORITY:*** Armed with devastating energy weapons powered by condensed lightning cores, neural enhancers, and planet-wide surveillance networks.
    *   Extreme individual speed, near-instantaneous reflex times, and unmatched solo combat proficiency.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Speed is absolute. Efficiency is law." ]***

### **~|| 9. THE METAL KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Order of Ores-}**

An industrial and architectural marvel constructed across a sprawling, rugged landscape of jagged metal peaks and deep canyons where the crust is overflowing with dense magnetic ores and exotic alloys.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Gleaming cities consisting of polished steel spires and heavy, interlocking geometric plates.
    *   Advanced magnetic levitation districts and heavy hydraulic systems powered directly by the planet's core eat.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Profoundly stoic, deeply disciplined, and unyielding to external pressure.
    *   Value structural logic, tradition, and exquisite craftsmanship above all else.
    *   Emotionally guarded, but maintain a fierce, unbreakable loyalty to their respective industrial Guilds.
    *   **The Ore Hierarchy:** Governed by a strict caste system known as "The Order of Ores." Social status is outwardly reflected by the quality and rarity of the metal alloy armor they wear. Societal change is intentionally slow.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   **Attrition & Weight:** Strategy focuses on absolute defense and massive crushing force. They never dodge what can be deflected, choosing to stand perfectly still until the enemy exhausts themselves before delivering a single, fight-ending blow. Every citizen must forge their own personalized armor as a sacred rite.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Staggering raw physical strength and peerless structural durability.
    *   Natural kinetic energy absorption, allowing them to grow sturdier from incoming physical blows.
    *   Inherent internal compass via highly developed magnetic senses; total immunity to standard physical fatigue.

> [!Quote] ***[ "The weapon may break, but the mountain remains. Steel is truth. All else is soft." ]***

​————————————————————————

### **~|| 10. THE FLORA/NATURE KINGDOM ||~**
**{-The Green-Lung Basin-}**

An immense, self-sustaining biosphere encompassing continent-sized jungles, gargantuan elder-trees, and crystalline flower fields. It represents the literal respiratory system of the planet, fiercely protecting the delicate balance of life.

*   ***GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE:***
    *   Biologically engineered cities constructed entirely from iron-hard wood and intricately woven vine bridges.
    *   Living tree-homes that naturally expand and grow alongside the family unit inhabiting them, illuminated by bioluminescent moss.

*   ***GENERAL BEHAVIOR:***
    *   Deeply nurturing, protective, and guided by an ancient, macro-perspective of time.
    *   Possess a literal spiritual connection to the planet's internal pulse. They operate on a strict circular economy where waste is non-existent.
    *   ***THE INNER PULSE:*** Communication is carried out via complex pheromone-speech or low-frequency root vibrations. Governed by the sacred Circle of Elders; private property does not exist within the realm.
    *   ***REALM HOSTILITY:*** While peaceful, they become terrifyingly aggressive and vengeful if their forests are threatened, harboring an absolute hatred for industrial pollution or dead technology.

*   ***COMBAT PHILOSOPHY:***
    *   ***ENTRAPMENT & TOXINS:*** Focuses on rapid environmental manipulation, entangling targets in iron-hard roots, and outlasting foes through superior cellular regeneration and toxic spores.

*   ***TRAITS & ADVANTAGES:***
    *   Near-instantaneous biological regeneration and total control over regional plant life and animal behavior.
    *   Absolute mastery over medicinal and lethal biochemical toxins.
    *   Flawless, perfect camouflage in any natural terrain.

> [!Quote] ***[ "Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished. To harm the leaf is to wound the root." ]***
