# **<(THE OMNIA ELEMENTAL PERIODICITY)>**

The global civilization of Planet Omnia operates at an industrial and socio-economic scale completely beyond mortal comprehension. To sustain a planet of this magnitude, the workforce and its respective elemental systems are systematically categorized into three distinct layers of reality.

### **< THE THREE ELEMENTAL CORE CATEGORIES >**
*   ***THE PRIMORDIAL CORES (The 8 Pillars):*** The foundational elements managing the physical, mechanical, and geographical systems of the planet.
*   ***THE ASCENDANT DIVINES (The 2 Reality-Benders):*** Rare, high-frequency elements capable of directly manipulating non-physical cosmic constants.
*   ***THE FUSED ELEMENTS (The 13 Synthesized Wholes):*** Specialized, womb-born syntheses created to permanently handle hyper-specific planetary maintenance.
