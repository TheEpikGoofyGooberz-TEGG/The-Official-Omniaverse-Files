### **ERA 1: THE PROLOGUE (THE AGE OF RADIANCE)**
*   **TEMPORAL WINDOW:** 
    *   **Solar Track Range:** Year 1 to 483,247,185,934,612 O.A. (Omnian Epoch)
    *   **System Registry:** Aevum 483,247, Track 185,934,612
*   **GLOBAL PARAMETERS & LORE EXPANSION:** 
    *   **The Automated Macro-System:** Planet Omnia operates at an absolute mechanical and spiritual peak. The passage of time is a literal physical gearset governed by a massive 36-hour rotation split flawlessly into 12 hours of high-frequency photon Radiance, 12 hours of atmospheric Twilight, and 12 hours of deep-freeze Shade. 
    *   **The Infrastructure Months:** Global equilibrium is maintained by the automated operational phases of the 8 Primordial Cores and 2 Ascendant Divines. For 34 days each, the core months systematically execute planetary maintenance: *Mensis Ignis* strokes the Great Furnaces; *Mensis Aqua* regulates the global hydraulic gates; *Mensis Terra* engages the Earth Titan for tectonic crust weaving; *Mensis Ferri* runs core engine diagnostics; *Mensis Florae* pulses biosphere agricultural growth; *Mensis Venti* balances wind corridors; *Mensis Fulguris* calibrates the global power grid; and *Mensis Glaciei* distributes deep thermal sinks. This is followed by the 35-day ascendant blocks of *Mensis Lucis* (solar charging) and *Mensis Inanis* (matrix anchoring).
    *   **The Weekly Rhythm:** Society organizes its labor around the 7-day automated engine pulse, starting with the mechanical initiation of *Dies Exordii*, maximizing power fields on *Dies Pulsus*, witnessing mineral land-shaping on *Dies Genesis*, clearing mantle pressure vents on *Dies Purgationis*, anchoring stabilizers on *Dies Columnarum*, and resting under safety protocols during the clear harvesting of *Dies Claritatis* and the deep-freeze stillness of *Dies Torporis*.
    *   **The Divine Holiday:** Every year concludes with the 23-day *Spatium Festivum* (*Mensis Natalis*). The sentient planet’s **Weird Core** effortlessly absorbs 100% of the structural and mechanical strain of reality with zero stress, allowing the Sentinel Titans, factories, and civilian work networks to completely shut down for an era of endless global feasts and absolute rest.
    *   **The Geopolitical Geographies:** Global civilizations thrive within distinct elemental territories, forming a vast linguistic and cultural tapestry. The **Everlasting Radiance Kingdom** serves as the ultimate geopolitical melting pot where all cultures and languages intersect. Surrounding it are the specialized sovereign states: the ancient, high-register *Void Kingdom* (Latin, Sumerian, Aramaic); the blazing *Fire Kingdom* (Japanese, Turkish, Thai); the hyper-conductive *Lightning Kingdom* (Chinese, Korean, Finnish); the crystalline *Ice Kingdom* (Polish, Icelandic, Māori); the oceanic *Water Kingdom* (Polynesian, Greek, Malay); the atmospheric *Air Kingdom* (French, Sanskrit, Irish); the continental *Earth Kingdom* (Spanish, Italian, Quechua); the dense *Flora Kingdom* (Swahili, Mayan, Hindi); and the industrial *Metal Kingdom* (German, Russian, Swedish).
*   **NARRATIVE TONE:** 
    *   Vibrant, hopeful, prosperous, and highly structured. While minor regional friction, mercantile disputes, and political maneuvering exist to keep the world grounded, there is no conceptual understanding of systemic collapse or global extinction.
*   **OC STRUCTURAL IMPACT:** 
    *   Inhabitants live highly structured, traditional lives within established guilds, military orders, or cultural networks. 
    *   Characters hone their primary or fused elemental abilities through disciplined, safe, and academic methods. Physical or psychological trauma is deeply personal (e.g., localized accidents, family rivalries) rather than a societal scar caused by global warfare.

### **ERA 2: THE GREAT CULLING (THE CONVERGENCE OF NULL)**
*   **TEMPORAL WINDOW:** 
    *   **Solar Track Range:** Year 483,247,185,934,612 to 483,247,185,934,691 O.A.
    *   **System Registry:** Aevum 483,247, Track 185,934,691 (A historic 79-Solar-Track macro-fracture initiated by the 79-Day planetary blitzkrieg).
*   **GLOBAL PARAMETERS & LORE EXPANSION:** 
    *   **The Onslaught of Null:** Axian unleashes a catastrophic, unprecedented invasion across the face of Omnia. His operational strategy bypasses standard planetary defenses by targeting the cornerstone of global unity: the **Everlasting Radiance Kingdom**. Over the course of the conflict, this massive cultural capital is systematically torn apart, firebombed, and brought to near-total ruin.
    *   **Systemic Corruption:** The automated clockwork systems of the planet are forcefully corrupted. The seasonal cycles of the Primordial Cores stall or wildly overlap.
    *   **Shattered Lineages:** Whole bloodlines, ancestral libraries, and regional kingdoms are permanently fractured. Populations are displaced across massive, hostile continental distances as geography shifts under erratic elemental localized surges.
*   **NARRATIVE TONE:** 
    *   Terrifying, chaotic, urgent, and apocalyptically dark. The atmosphere is defined by sudden, staggering loss, the gory reality of total war, and the utter desperation of localized populations trying to survive against an overwhelming, unreasoning cosmic force.
*   **OC STRUCTURAL IMPACT:** 
    *   This era serves as the definitive point of origin for profound physical and psychological trauma across the population. OCs are directly impacted by Axian's interference—watching their ancestral homes burn, experiencing the shattering of their families, or suffering permanent physical scarring.
    *   The pure flow of Elemental Energy becomes deeply corrupted by trauma. Elemental control is erratic, volatile, and dangerously unstable, triggered unpredictably by pure adrenaline, grief, terror, and raw survival instincts rather than structured training.

### **ERA 3: THE DARK AGE (THE RESILIENCE OF SURVIVORS)**
*   **TEMPORAL WINDOW:** 
    *   **Solar Track Range:** Year 483,247,185,934,691 to [AWAITING CHRONO-CALIBRATION]
    *   **System Registry:** [SYSTEM LOGS CURRENTLY ACTIVE / LIVE REWRITES IN PROGRESS]
*   **GLOBAL PARAMETERS & LORE EXPANSION:** 
    *   **The Fractured Frontier:** In the immediate, bleak wake of the Culling, the global landscape is unrecognizable. The grand mass-communication lines and automated transit rails connecting the kingdoms are completely severed. Continental communities are isolated into distinct, hyper-vigilant pockets surrounded by corrupted elemental dead zones or rogue warbands.
    *   **The Agonizing Rebirth:** Proving the stubborn resilience of the world, survivors refuse to fade away. The ruined Everlasting Radiance Kingdom undergoes a slow, grueling, but miraculous full recovery. Though scarred and heavily fortified, it stands tall once more as a beacon of cross-cultural hope. In the shadows of the broken outer kingdoms, independent sub-factions, local survival guilds, scavenger networks, and rogue defense cells organize to adapt day-to-day life to a permanently altered environment.
*   **NARRATIVE TONE:** 
    *   Grim, gritty, cautious, and highly localized, but underscored by a fierce, unyielding spirit of resilience, tactical survival, and quiet psychological recovery.
*   **OC STRUCTURAL IMPACT:** 
    *   Characters in this era are battle-hardened, cynical, or deeply traumatized neutral survivors, rogue heroes, or predatory antagonists. 
    *   Day-to-day survival requires intense resource management. Characters must navigate regional ruins with forged equipment, specialized custom weapons, and armor made of scarce elemental materials. OCs must carefully manage their finite energy reserves while actively processing the severe psychological fallout and triggers left behind by the horrors of Era 2.

### **ERA 4: THE ENDGAME (THE CLIMAX OF FATE)**
*   **TEMPORAL WINDOW:** 
    *   **Solar Track Range:** [AWAITING HIGH-LEVEL COORDINATE ACTIVATION]
    *   **System Registry:** [RESERVED FOR CLIMACTIC FRAME-ENGAGEMENT]
*   **GLOBAL PARAMETERS & LORE EXPANSION:** 
    *   **The War of Extinction:** The long-brewing cold war of the survival factions reaches a violent boiling point. The pieces are permanently set across the global chessboard: the ideological factions allied with Axian's vision of a completely purged matrix clash in absolute, uncompromising warfare against the allied heroes fighting to protect the remaining soul of Planet Omnia.
    *   **Environmental Strain:** The scale of the war pushes the metaphysics of Omnia to its absolute breaking point. Combatants unleash unrestricted, high-level Elemental Energy, custom forged artifacts, and cataclysmic **Spectral Affinity Forms** simultaneously across multiple front lines. This extreme concentration of power fractures local spatial anchors, tearing the planetary crust and threatening to permanently destabilize the baseline matrix of reality.
*   **NARRATIVE TONE:** 
    *   High-stakes, epic, breathless, and deeply tragic. Every skirmish, duel, and tactical decision carries the binary weight of absolute, permanent extinction or ultimate, hard-fought salvation.
*   **OC STRUCTURAL IMPACT:** 
    *   This is the absolute destination of your character's journey. The specific dream, drive, or core objective they have fought for since the ruins of the Culling reaches its definitive realization or tragic end.
    *   There is no longer any room for hesitation or fear. Characters are pushed to their absolute mathematical limits; their psychological trauma must either be fully mastered, conquered, and weaponized into unbreakable focus, or it will cause their immediate physical downfall as all powers and custom gear are tested to the brink of destruction.

### **ERA 5: THE EPILOGUE (THE ERA OF FULL RECOVERY)**
* **TEMPORAL WINDOW:** 
    * **Solar Track Range:** [AWAITING FINAL ARCHIVAL GENERATION]
    * **System Registry:** [ARCHIVAL LOCK PENDING CORE DATA VERIFICATION]
* **GLOBAL PARAMETERS & LORE EXPANSION:** 
    * **The Great Stitching:** The grand war concludes, the immediate threat is successfully beaten back, and the planet is allowed to finally breathe. This triggers a monumental, miraculous global recovery across all continents. The severe physical, environmental, and metaphysical wounds inflicted upon the crust, the atmosphere, and the core matrix heal completely.
    * **The Interconnected Golden Age:** Out of the ashes of isolation, the global kingdoms rebuild. They emerge stronger, more technologically advanced, and more structurally interconnected than at any point in pre-Culling history, bound together by the shared trauma of survival.
    * **The Heavy Truth:** Beneath the vibrant celebrations lies a chilling, calculated reality: the threat is only temporarily vanquished, and this beautiful peace has a definitive expiration date. Global leaders, elite survival guilds, and the rulers of the kingdoms do not waste this prosperity; they use every day of the recovery era to systematically, meticulously stockpile resources, map out defenses, and train armies for the inevitable day the Null returns to finish the job.
* **NARRATIVE TONE:** 
    * Grandly triumphant yet deeply vigilant; profoundly hopeful but intensely strategic, protective, and far-sighted.
* **OC STRUCTURAL IMPACT:** 
    * Characters experience the profound historical payoff of their survival, shifting their day-to-day focus from raw combat to the preservation and fortification of the world they bled to save.
    * Heroes, survivors, and former antagonists utilize this era of abundance to finally heal their physical scars and deeply resolve their psychological traumas. They pass down their ancestral and elemental knowledge to the next generation, establishing new sub-factions, localized elite defense guilds, and regional security measures. Whether an OC chooses to spend these days enjoying a hard-earned, peaceful civilian life or stands as a watchful, armor-clad guardian training the youth, their legacy is officially cemented and archived in the eternal history of a thriving, watchful planet.
