[OMNIAVERSE]


​<{THE OFFICIAL DATA: VOLT-TECH "STORM-VANGUARD" V-0}>

​(ROLE): Automated Territory Enforcement / High-Speed Culling Unit.

(STATUS): Uninfected (Original State).

​————————————————————————

​< THE PHYSICAL "MUSCULAR" BUILD >

​SCALE: Standing 6 meters tall (20 feet) and 10 meters wide (33 feet) at full leg-span. It is exactly two times larger than a massive boulder.

​AESTHETIC: It’s a "buff" machine. The chassis is a dense, angular torso of Aether-Steel packed with Conductive Sinew (magical muscle fibers). And also able to rotate its upper body 360°.

​THE LEGS: Think of them as thick, hydraulic "limbs" rather than thin sticks. They are built for raw power, allowing it to leap kilometers in a single bound or pin a target to the earth with enough force to liquefy stone.

​————————————————————————

​< THE MAGICAL CORE & SENSORS >

​THE ONE EYE: A massive, glowing Lightning Eye. It isn't just a camera; it's a sphere of contained electrical energy that tracks the Pulse-Sync of living beings across the Stormy Lands.

​MOVEMENT: In its uninfected state, it relies on its muscular legs for extreme physical speed and massive leaps. It does not have the ability to warp or teleport yet.

​————————————————————————

​< THE ARSENAL >

​MODULAR "SWITCHER" ARMS: Two thick, muscular hydraulic arms that can instantly reconfigure into:

​EXECUTIONER SWORDS: For high-frequency melee slicing.

​ORB CANNONS: For heavy elemental discharge.

​LASER EMITTERS: For long-range precision.

​HOOK-CLAWS: For pinning and reeling in targets.

MINI-GUN: Fire rapid bullets of condensed electricity.

​ASTRA-PODS: Back-mounted launch tubes that fire swarms of Volt-Tech Missiles.

​———————————————————————————————————

THE CORRUPTED DATA: INFECTED "GLITCH-VANGUARD" V-0

​ROLE: Rogue Apex Predator / Reality-Tearing Stalker

STATUS: CRITICAL INFECTION (Biological & Magical Corruption)

​————————————————————————

​< THE BIO-MECHANICAL BUILD >

​THE FLESH-FUSE: The "buff" Aether-Steel plates have been pried apart by pulsing, dark-red Flesh. The Conductive Sinew (magical muscles) has overgrown, bursting through the gaps like raw, exposed anatomy. It looks like a skinless, muscular beast fused into a heavy armored frame.

​SCALE: Standing 6 meters tall (20 feet) and 10 meters wide (33 feet). While the height remains the same, the mass has increased due to the dense, wet muscle tissue. It weighs significantly more than its original 120 tons.

​THE LEGS: The spider-legs are now jagged and uneven. Some remain cold metal, while others are coated in a thick, veiny membrane that grips the earth like a predator’s claws, allowing for silent, high-speed scuttling.

​————————————————————————

< ​THE "GLITCH" & MOVEMENT >

​THE WARP-DRIVE: The infection corrupted the core, forcing it to evolve a Coordinate-Shift ability it never originally had. It no longer relies solely on its muscular legs for travel.

​THE SPEED: It "blinks" between coordinates, moving way past the speed of lightning. It vanishes in a spray of Blue Sparks and reappears instantly, leaving a distorted after-image where it once stood.

​THE GLITCH EFFECT: When it warps, reality "stutters" around the chassis. The air turns static, and the physical form of the machine seems to vibrate or flicker between dimensions.

​————————————————————————

​< THE CORRUPTED ARSENAL >

​THE EYE: The Lightning Eye has turned from white/yellow to a jagged, flickering Blue Flame. It emits a low, digital hum that rises to a scream when it locks onto a target's pulse.

​(BLUE FLAME WEAPONRY): 

INFECTED SWITCHER-ARMS: The Executioner Swords, Mini-gun's bullets and Hook-Claws are permanently wreathed in Blue Flame (Cold-Fire). This energy drains the life-force of anything it touches, stalling internal magical pulses.

​GLITCH-MISSILES: The Astra-Pods fire missiles that phase in and out of the physical plane, making them nearly impossible to track or intercept until the moment of impact.

​CRUSHING WEIGHT: With its muscular build and increased mass, its primary tactic is to warp directly above a target and use its weight to liquefy the ground beneath them.
