### **< III. THE NANOSECOND OF ERASURE >**

*   ***THE PRINCE'S PATROL:*** Miles beyond the border, across the stormy plains of the Lightning Kingdom, Prince Zakari "Zaki" Léi Zhèn (21) moved through the vibrant green grass with the low, predatory carriage of a seasoned master swordsman. Firmly in his hand was his *Techno Volt-Sword*, its divine blade humming with a high-voltage rhythmic charge. Encasing his physical form was a shimmering, protective *Light Spell* cast by foreign Light Elementians—a vital firewall keeping Axian's ten-year-old plague from overwriting his soul.

* ***THE CHRONO-DESYNC:*** The atmosphere detonated. Blue sparks tore through the air as the *Infected Storm-Vanguard V-0* glitched into reality. 120 tons of muscular, infected flesh and rusted Aether-Steel slammed into the bedrock, throwing Zaki skyward. In a flicker of frame-skipping movement, the V-0 coordinate-shifted, throwing a jagged metal Hook-Claw through the rain. The iron bit deep into Zaki’s shoulder, pinning him into the mud as a corrupted vortex of Blue Flame began to surge down the links of the chain. Suddenly, a big Humanoid-shape robot appeared behind the Storm-Vanguard in a blink with lightning trails, the thing being the Infected Lightning Titan, ***ASTRAPIUS***.
