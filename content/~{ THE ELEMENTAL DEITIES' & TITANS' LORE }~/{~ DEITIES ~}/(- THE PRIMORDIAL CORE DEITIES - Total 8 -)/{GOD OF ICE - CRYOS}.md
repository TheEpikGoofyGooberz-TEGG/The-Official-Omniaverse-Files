### **{- I. CRYOS/ZEN THE GOD OF ICE -}**5

One of the 23 Deities, Zen is the Being who bears the very concept of Ice. He has access to every single Ice-related moves, abilities, powers, etc.

*   ***THE MELTING OF ICE:*** From the 1st Timeline, Zen died by trying to bring his Infected Titan (Cryolith) back into his control.
  Now in the 2nd Timeline, all the Deities retained their memories from the 1st timeline, and Zen remembered he died from what he did, and knew to never try attempting it again for the Titans have been Overclocked by the Blue Flame with void mastery from the 1st timeline Axian siphoned.

### **{- II. WEAPONS -}**

*   ***SCYTHE OF CRYOS:*** Zen's Scythe, it was manifested from Zen's element. Its blade is soo sharp, it's capable of slicing through even the most strongest things with ease.

### **{- III. HALO OF DIVINE ICE -}**

This Halo was granted to Zen by Light Radiance for his achievements. His element covered the Halo (Pure Divine Light) in Ice, making the HALO OF DIVINE ICE.The Halo is sentient, and it helps Zen.
