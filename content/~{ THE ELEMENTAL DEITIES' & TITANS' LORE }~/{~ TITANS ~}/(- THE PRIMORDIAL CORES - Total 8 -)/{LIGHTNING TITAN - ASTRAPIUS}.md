***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Living Weapon of Storm and Steel-*

***--FORM:*** A high-tech humanoid body powered by lightning.
•
***--SIZE:*** 2nd smallest Titan — 3.4× larger than an average Elementian.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--NEURAL STRIKE:*** Every hit with its dual blades disrupts the electrical signals in an enemy's body, causing temporary paralysis or "lag" in Titan-grade pilots.

***--RELATIVIST REFLEXES:*** His perception of time slows as his energy levels rise, allowing him to weave through thousands of projectiles as if they were standing still.
