#### **~||FIRE TITAN: IGNILITH||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Wrath of Living Magma-*

***--FORM:*** A colossal entity made of massive floating jagged red flaming magma slabs and boulders in a Humanoid-shape.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** Pieces do not touch but are held together by ropes of liquid fire.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--VOLCANIC PROPULSION:*** Can launch its limbs as meteoric projectiles, retracting them instantly via fire-tethers.
***--DESTRUCTION FEEDING:*** Thrives in high-pressure environments; becomes faster and stronger the more it destroys.
***--THERMAL AURA:*** Passively melts incoming projectiles before they can make physical contact.
***--MAGMA SURGE:*** Can liquefy the ground beneath it to create localized eruptions.
•
*--Extremely aggressive and relentless.*

> [!Quote] ***[ Ignilith does not reason; it burns. ]***
