#### **~||WATER TITAN: IMMENSIMAR||~**
***{Creator: AQUARIA/YEN}***
*-The Leviathan of the Deep-*

***--FORM:*** An immense aquatic leviathan.
•
***--DOMAIN:*** The deepest oceans of Omnia.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--ABYSSAL PRESSURE:*** Can create water spheres with the crushing pressure of the deep ocean trench.
***--LIQUID FLUIDITY:*** Can compress its massive body to fit through small openings or evade strikes.
***--HYDRO-KINETIC MASTERY:*** Controls the tides and currents; can summon tsunamis with a flick of its tail.
***--ETERNAL ENDURANCE:*** As long as it is in contact with water, its stamina and strength are infinite.

*--Tempered, patient, and overwhelming.*
