#### **~||FLORA/NATURE TITAN: FLORAGANTE||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Weaver of the Living World-*

***--FORM:*** A colossal entity made of massive slabs and chunks of all kinds of natural materials that they changed into (E.g., Iron-hard wood) and is covered in flora.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** Pieces aren't physically touching but bounded by pulsing ropes of green life-energy in a shape of a wide muscular human.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--BIOSPHERE INTEGRATION:*** Can communicate with and command every living plant and animal on the planet.
***--TECTONIC ROOTS:*** Its root system can shift tectonic plates or crush underground bunkers.
***--INFINITE SPORE CLOUD:*** Releases pollens that can either heal allies or paralyze the nervous systems of enemies.
***--LIVING FORTRESS:*** Its body is harder than refined steel and regenerates faster than it can be cut.
•
*--Protective, nurturing, and ancient.*

> [!Quote] ***[ Floragante is the pulse of the world. ]***
