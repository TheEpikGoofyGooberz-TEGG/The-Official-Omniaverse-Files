#### **~||METAL TITAN: SIDEROGANTE||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Unyielding Colossus of Ore-*

***--FORM:*** A massive, tiered humanoid composed of interlocking plates of raw iron, polished steel, and reinforced alloys.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A core of hyper-dense molten metal that acts as a hydraulic system for its heavy plating.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--INDUSTRIAL EVOLUTION:*** Capable of absorbing nearby metallic materials to repair its frame or increase its physical mass.
***--GODLY TENSILE STRENGTH:*** Its limbs can exert pressure equal to a planetary core, crushing any known material.
***--KINETIC REDIRECTION:*** Its surface is angled perfectly to cause physical strikes to slide off or shatter upon impact.
***--MALLEABLE ARSENAL:*** Can instantly reshape its limbs into high-frequency blades, drills, or impenetrable shields.
***--INTERNAL FORGE:*** Can superheat its own external plates to deal searing thermal-metal damage on contact.
•
*--Stoic, unmoving, and absolute.*
