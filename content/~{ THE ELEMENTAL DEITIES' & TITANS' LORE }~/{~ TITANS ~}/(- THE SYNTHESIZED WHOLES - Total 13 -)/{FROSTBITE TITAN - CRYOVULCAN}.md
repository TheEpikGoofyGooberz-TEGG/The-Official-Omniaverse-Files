#### **~||FROSTBITE TITAN: CRYOVULCAN||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Endothermic Calamity-*

***--FORM:*** A jagged, terrifying construct of black ice and glowing neon-blue acid.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A chemical core that simultaneously freezes and dissolves matter, encased in a shell of "Ever-Cold" obsidian-ice.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--THERMAL SIPHON:*** Passively removes all heat from a 5-mile radius, turning the air into a vacuum that makes breathing and movement nearly impossible for organic life.
***--CORROSIVE SUB-ZERO TOUCH:*** Its acidic components melt through Titan-grade armor while the cold instantly turns the pilot or the core into a brittle, frozen statue.
***--CRYO-REGENERATION:*** Absorbs heat energy from enemy attacks (like Fire or Plasma) to rapidly regrow lost limbs or armor plates.
***--GODLY MAGICAL POTENCY:*** Can manifest "Frozen Rain"—shards of acidic ice that fall with the force of meteorites.
•
*--Cold, calculating, and predatory.*
