### **~|| LAVA TITAN: MAGMAGANTE ||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Living Flow of the Mantle-*

***--FORM:*** A massive, hulking entity of shifting, glowing orange liquid stone under a cooling black crust.
• ​
***--STRUCTURE:*** A "Convection Core" that keeps the stone in a permanent state of liquid-solid transition, allowing it to expand or contract its size.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--UNCONTAINABLE PERSISTENCE:*** Moves like a slow-motion tidal wave; it cannot be stopped by physical barriers as it simply melts or flows around them.
***--MOLTEN DOMAIN:*** Can liquefy any terrain it touches, turning a forest or city into a boiling lake of magma within minutes.
***--EXTREME DENSITY:*** Possesses the highest raw physical weight of all Titans, making its footsteps create localized earthquake tremors.
***--HEAT-SHROUD AURA:*** Emits a field of superheated air that vaporizes incoming projectiles and missiles before they can make contact with its surface.
•
*--Patient, relentless, and stoic.*

> [!Quote] ***[ Magmagante does not strike; it simply occupies the space where you once stood. ]***
