#### **~||SOLAR TITAN: SOL-INVICTUS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Beacon of Radiance-*

***--FORM:*** A tall, slender figure composed of white fire and gold light, wearing an armor of "Solidified Radiance."
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A central core of solar energy bound by rings of pure photons that rotate at the speed of light.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--PURIFYING RADIATION:*** Emits a constant field of high-frequency light that cleanses the "Infection" and corruption from nearby allies or environments.
***--RELATIVISTIC MOVEMENT:*** The third fastest Titan overall; it can move in "Light-Leaps," appearing to teleport by traveling as a beam of pure energy.
***--PHOTONIC WEAPONRY:*** Attacks are purely energetic, ignoring physical defenses and shields to strike directly at the enemy’s internal core.
***--BLINDING REVELATION:*** Can release a burst of "Absolute Light" that reveals hidden enemies (like Phantasma) and permanently disables optical sensors.
•
*--Noble, distant, and blinding.*
