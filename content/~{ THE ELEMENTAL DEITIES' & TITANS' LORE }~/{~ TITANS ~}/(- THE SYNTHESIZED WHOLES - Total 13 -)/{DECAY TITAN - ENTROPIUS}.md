#### **~||DECAY TITAN: ENTROPIUS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Witness of Time-*

***--FORM:*** A skeletal, gaunt figure made of shifting sands, withered obsidian, and ancient, crumbling bandages.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A core of Air and Earth energy that accelerates molecular vibration to the point of structural failure.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--PASSIVE EROSION:*** Emits a "Field of Atrophy" that causes any inorganic material—steel, stone, or glass—to rust, crack, and turn to dust within seconds of proximity.
***--DISSOLUTION STRIKE:*** Its attacks don't just cause physical damage; they turn solid defenses into fine sand upon impact, making traditional shields useless.
***--SANDSTORM EVASION:*** Can dissolve its physical form into a cloud of abrasive, high-speed desert sand to reposition or bypass enemy attacks.
***--TEMPORAL DECAY:*** Moves with an eerie, staggering rhythm that makes it appear to be moving in slow motion, yet it "jumps" forward in time to strike before the enemy can react.
***--INEVITABILITY:*** Capable of "withering" the mana out of magical spells, causing incoming energy attacks to fizzle and fade before they reach its body.
•
*--Patient, somber, and inevitable.*

> [!Quote] ***[ Entropius does not destroy; it simply brings the end sooner. ]***
