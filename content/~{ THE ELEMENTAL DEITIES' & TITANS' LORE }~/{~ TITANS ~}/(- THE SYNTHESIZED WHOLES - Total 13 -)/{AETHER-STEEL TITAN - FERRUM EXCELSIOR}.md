#### **~||AETHER-STEEL TITAN: FERRUM EXCELSIOR||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Weightless Fortress-*

***--FORM:*** A sleek, reflective knight made of living Aether-Steel.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** Shifting metal plates that adapt to any combat style, held together by a core of stabilized anti-gravity energy.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--GRAVITY NEGATION:*** Operates in a state of constant weightlessness, allowing it to move with a speed and grace impossible for its massive size; it can stand on a single leaf without bending it.
***--ADAPTIVE GEOMETRY:*** Can transform its limbs into any weapon instantly—claymores, lances, or massive tower shields—with zero delay between transitions.
***--SELF-REFINING ALLOY:*** The more it is struck, the harder its metal becomes; the "living steel" analyzes the kinetic frequency of the enemy's strength and adapts its molecular density to counter it.
***--MIRROR-SHEEN DEFLECTION:*** Its surface is polished to a degree that it can reflect energy-based attacks (like Light or Plasma) back at the attacker with 100% efficiency.
***--AETHER-VIBRATION:*** Can vibrate its blades at such a high frequency that they cut through the atomic bonds of any material, bypassing physical durability entirely.
•
*--Stoic, honorable, and unbreakable.*
