#### **~||STORM TITAN: TYPHONIS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Cataclysmic Eye-*

***--FORM:*** A towering, shifting vortex of storm clouds and rain.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A solid core of condensed air with lightning spiraling through its "skin."
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--BAROMETRIC DOMINATION:*** Can manipulate local air pressure to crush enemies into the ground or pull them into the sky.
***--KINETIC HARVESTING:*** Absorbs the friction of the wind and moving objects to generate infinite electrical energy.
***--EYE OF THE STORM:*** The center of its body is a zone of absolute calm where it can trap and "process" targets.
***--AERO-STATIC FLIGHT:*** Moves with chaotic, high-speed unpredictability, leaving vacuum trails that collapse with explosive force.
•
*--Fierce, loud, and domineering.*
