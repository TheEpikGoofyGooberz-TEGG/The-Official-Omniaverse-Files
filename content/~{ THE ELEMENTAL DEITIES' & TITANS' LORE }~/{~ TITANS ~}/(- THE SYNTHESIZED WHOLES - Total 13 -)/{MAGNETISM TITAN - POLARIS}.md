#### **~||MAGNETISM TITAN: POLARIS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Master of the Iron Tide-*

***--FORM:*** A giant geode of magnetic ores with floating iron sand orbiting it.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A core of pure electromagnetic energy acting as a heart.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--POLAR REVERSAL:*** Can flip the magnetic polarity of an enemy, causing them to be violently repelled or attracted to the earth.
***--IRON-SAND SWARM:*** Controls a cloud of metallic dust that can act as a shield, a grinding saw, or a billion tiny needles.
***--AXIAL FLIGHT:*** Can "walk" through the air by tethering itself to the planet's magnetic ley lines.
***--WEAPON SEIZURE:*** Can remotely rip metal weapons from the hands of opponents or turn them against their owners.
•
*--Calculating, calm, and attractive*
