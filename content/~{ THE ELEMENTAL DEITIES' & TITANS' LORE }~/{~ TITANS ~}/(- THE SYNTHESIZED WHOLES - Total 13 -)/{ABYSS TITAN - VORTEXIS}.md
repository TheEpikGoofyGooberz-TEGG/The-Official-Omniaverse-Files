#### **~||ABYSS TITAN: VORTEXIS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Gravity of the Deep-*

***--FORM:*** A swirling, liquid-dark humanoid that pulls light into its center.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A core of Void energy within a shell of high-pressure "heavy water."
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--EVENT HORIZON:*** Generates a gravitational field that slows time and movement for everyone except itself.
***--CRUSHING DEPTHS:*** Its physical strikes apply the weight of a thousand fathoms, flattening enemies instantly.
***--DARK MATTER INTANGIBILITY:*** Can momentarily slip into a sub-atomic state to let attacks pass through its body.
***--SINGULARITY PULL:*** Can create mini-black holes that suck in nearby projectiles and environment.
•
*--Silent, crushing, and absolute.*
