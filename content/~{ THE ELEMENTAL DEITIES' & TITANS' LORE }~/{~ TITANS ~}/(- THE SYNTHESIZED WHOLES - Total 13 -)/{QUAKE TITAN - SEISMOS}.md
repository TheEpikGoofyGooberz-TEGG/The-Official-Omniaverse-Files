#### **~||QUAKE TITAN: SEISMOS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Resonance of the Deep-*

***--FORM:*** A heavy, wide construct of obsidian plates and vibrating, humming crystals.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** Tectonic plates held together by high-intensity Sound magic and gravity-well anchors.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--RESONANT LIQUEFACTION:*** Can vibrate the ground at such a specific frequency that solid earth turns to liquid, swallowing armies or other Titans.
***--IMPACT MULTIPLICATION:*** Every physical strike it lands generates a secondary seismic wave that travels through the enemy’s body, causing internal structural failure.
***--UNSTOPPABLE MOMENTUM:*** Once it begins a charge, its "Godly Durability" and mass make it impossible to stagger or halt by any physical force.
***--TECTONIC SHIFT:*** Can force the earth to rise or fall by miles, creating instant mountains or chasms to reshape the battlefield.
•
*--Heavy, resonant, and certain.*
