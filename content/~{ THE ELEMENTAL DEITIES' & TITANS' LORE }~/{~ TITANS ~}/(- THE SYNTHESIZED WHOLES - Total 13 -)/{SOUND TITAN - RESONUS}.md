#### **~||SOUND TITAN: RESONUS||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Silent Bell of Ruin-*

***--FORM:*** A humanoid construct of floating crystalline tuning forks.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** Held together by visible, high-frequency sound waves.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--INTERNAL RESONANCE:*** Can match the frequency of any material, causing armor or bones to shatter without a physical touch.
***--SONIC BLINK:*** Moves at the speed of sound, appearing as a blurred vibration before reappearing elsewhere.
***--ACOUSTIC NULLIFICATION:*** Can create a "Dead Zone" of total silence where no sound or vibration can exist, paralyzing those who rely on senses.
***--ECHO-LOCATION MASTERY:*** Can "see" through solid objects by reading the bounce-back of invisible frequencies.
•
*--Methodical, eerie, and precise.*
