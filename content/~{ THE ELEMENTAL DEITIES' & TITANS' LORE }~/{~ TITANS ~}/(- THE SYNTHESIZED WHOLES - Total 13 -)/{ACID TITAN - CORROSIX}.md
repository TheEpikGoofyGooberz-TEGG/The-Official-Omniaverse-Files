#### **~||ACID TITAN: CORROSIX||~**
***{Creator: Unnamed}***
*-The Dissolver of Wills-*

***--FORM:*** A shifting, translucent humanoid of glowing neon liquid.
•
***--STRUCTURE:*** A pressurized mana-glass frame containing a volatile core.
•
**(TRAITS):**
***--MOLECULAR UNBINDING:*** Its touch dissolves the bonds between atoms, turning solid steel into a puddle in seconds.
***--CAPILLARY PHASING:*** Can liquefy its entire form to travel through cracks, porous ground, or even an enemy’s armor seams.
***--CAUSTIC MISTS:*** Can vent pressurized acid gas that creates a fog of total decomposition.
***--ANALYTICAL DECAY:*** Can "taste" the composition of an enemy through its liquid, finding their physical weaknesses instantly.
•
*--Relentless, analytical, and cold.*

> [!Quote] ***[ Corrosix does not wound; it unmasks the atoms of its prey. ]***
