# **<{~THE ELEMENTAL TITANS~}>**
​
Colossal guardians (dozens of km tall) and physical manifestations of their Deities' element.

***THE ANCHORS:*** The Titans serve as the literal physical stabilizers for Omnia’s 536 billion-fold mass. Without their constant intervention, the sheer gravitational pull of a world 1.6 million times more massive than the Sun would cause it to ignite into a Hypermassive Quasi-Star or a Direct-Collapse Black Hole. It’s much scarier than a "standard" big star.

***TECHNICAL CHECK:*** A Blue Supergiant is a specific stage of a very high-mass star. Given Omnia's mass of 536 billion Earths (which is roughly 1.6 million times the mass of the Sun), it wouldn't just be a star; it would be one of the most massive stars ever conceived, likely hitting the Eddington Limit where its own light would blow it apart.

***GRAVITY:*** At this scale, Omnia's natural gravity is 8,126g.

***(THE TITANS):*** By negating 99.987% of this gravity, they maintain a 1g environment.

***(OMNIA'S SIZE VS. EARTH'S SUN):*** Omnia is 74 times wider than the Sun and has the mass of 1.6 million Suns.

***(THE SENTINELS):*** They were the primary protectors of their nations, shielding them from disasters and maintaining the balance of their respective lands.

***(THE POWER OF THE ANCHOR):*** To negate 99.987% of the natural 8,126g down to a habitable 1g, the Titans perform a feat of constant energy output that dwarfs the cumulative production of a thousand galaxies.

These beings do not merely stand upon the surface; they function as Gravitational Heat Sinks. As physical manifestations of their Deities, they act as cosmic conduits, piping the excess gravitational potential energy of Omnia out into the Omniverse to prevent the planet from igniting into a star.

When subjected to the 1000% Overclock, a Titan—already capable of stabilizing a 536-billion-Earth-mass world—effectively becomes a walking "Localized Quasar," a localized eruption of primordial force.

### **<(THE GRAVITATIONAL UMBILICAL: THE CORE-LOCK PROTOCOL)>**

To exist on a world with a natural gravity of 8,126g, the Titans cannot rely on physical structural integrity alone. Their presence is a feat of Active Spatial Anchoring.

**< 1. THE MECHANIC: Sub-Spatial Redirection >**

Titans do not "stand" on the planetary crust of Omnia. Instead, they project an invisible concentrated beam of gravitational force—the Umbilical—that extends from their center of mass, through the crust, and locks directly into the Condensed Core of the planet.

***PRESSURE DISPLACEMENT:*** 100% of the Titan’s moon-tier mass is diverted through this tether.

***THE "Zero-G" FOOTPRINT:*** Because their weight is being "pulled" toward the core through a sub-spatial channel rather than pushing "down" on the dirt, a Titan standing on a flower wouldn't even crush the petals.

***THE STABILIZER:*** This tether acts as a two-way street. It allows the Titans to "siphon" the excess gravitational energy of Omnia and vent it into the Omniverse, preventing the planet from collapsing into a Black Hole.

​————————————————————————

### **< 2. THE AXIAN CORRUPTION: "The Tether-Snap" >**

Under the 1000% Overclock, Axian has weaponized this safety feature. He has forced the Titans to reverse the polarity of their Umbilicals.

***THE OVERLOAD:*** Instead of venting energy into the core, the Titans are now violently sucking primordial energy out of the core to power their 10x Ascension.

***THE SURFACE CONSEQUENCE:*** The "Zero-G" footprint is gone. The Umbilical has become unstable. As a Titan moves, the tether "drags" through the crust like a white-hot wire through wax.

***THE DISASTER:*** This is why every step creates a Magnitude-14 earthquake. The crust isn't just being crushed; it is being atomized because the Titan’s moon-tier mass is finally hitting the floor for the first time in eons.
