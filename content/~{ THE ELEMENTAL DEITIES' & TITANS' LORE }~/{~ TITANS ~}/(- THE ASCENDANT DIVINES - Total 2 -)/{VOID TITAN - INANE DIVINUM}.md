### **~||VOID TITAN: INANE DIVINUM||~**
***{Creator: SHADOW SHADE/UMBRA UMBRA}***
*-The Forgotten Final Test-*

*   ***FORM:*** A Pitch Black Elementian Silhouette with White blinding eyes.
*   ***LOCATION:*** Resides solely within the Void Realm.
*   ***STATUS:*** Erased from all known records.
*   ***PURPOSE:*** Serves as the Final Trial of Void Mastery.

*   **(TRAITS)**
    *   ***FLAWLESS MIMICRY:*** It acts as a perfect dark mirror. Every motion the opponent makes is instantly calculated and reflected, ensuring the Titan is always the opponent's equal.
    *   ***EXISTENCE ERASURE:*** To lose is to cease. Those who fail the trial do not die; they are un-written from the timeline as if they never existed.
    *   ***ZERO-POINT INTERACTION:*** Within the Titan’s presence, the "noise" of the world is silenced. Armor, magic, and external weapons lose their meaning; only raw essence can interact with it. Only weapons, armor and magic connected to the Primordial Void is not silenced.
    *   ***DISCIPLINE ENFORCEMENT:*** The Titan exerts a crushing mental pressure. Any stray thought or lack of focus manifests as physical pain, forcing the opponent into a state of absolute, singular intent.
    *   ****MOMENTUM CULPABILITY:*** Punishes Greed. If the opponent strikes with too much malice or over-extends their reach, the Titan absorbs the force and sends it back through the opponent's own bones.
    *   ***LATENCY TAX:*** Punishes Slowness. A delay in thought or action siphons the opponent's vital speed, leaving them trapped in a slowed state while the Titan moves at the speed of thought.
    *   ***RHYTHM BREAKER:*** Punishes Disharmony. Striking or dodging a breath too early or late causes a "Desync," making the opponent’s body feel alien and unresponsive to their own will.
    *   ***VACUUM ADAPTATION:*** Punishes Hesitation. The Void abhors a vacuum; if the opponent tries to create distance or stall, the Titan collapses space to force a point-blank confrontation.
    *   ***ADAPTIVE ANTICIPATION:*** Punishes Predictability. Should the opponent rely on a single pattern, the Titan ceases to mimic and begins to pre-empt, waiting at the end of the next move before it begins.
    *   ***COMPOSURE DRAIN:*** Punishes Panic. When the opponent loses their calm, the Titan feeds on that chaotic energy, rapidly draining their stamina until they find their center or collapse.
    *   ***SYMPATHETIC ASCENSION:*** The Titan seeks a True Fight. As the opponent pushes their limits, the Titan’s power scales in perfect synchronization, staying exactly 1% ahead to force constant evolution.
    *   ***SOUL-STRIPPING:*** As the duel intensifies, the Titan shuts down the opponent's senses—sight, then sound, then touch—until they are fighting entirely through their connection to the Void.

*   **(THE CONSEQUENCE):**
    ***[ PROTOCOL: FINAL CORRECTION ]***
     Triggered by any lapse in speed, timing, or discipline. The Titan enters a state of absolute execution, delivering a 12-strike sequence that un-knits the target's soul from reality.

*   **(THE CONCLUSION)**
    ***[ THE ASCENSION TRIGGER ]***
     Inane Divinum does not yield. It continues to escalate until the opponent is forced to break their own fundamental limits to survive. Only when the opponent achieves a victory through pure, perfected Void Mastery does the Titan cease its assault.

     Upon defeat, the silhouette stands down and remains as a silent monument in the Void. It finally grants the opponent Unrestricted Access to their full potential, unlocking the "Prime" power that was previously locked behind the trial.

*Cold, impartial, and absolute.*

> [!Quote] ***[ To conquer the Void, you must surpass the Reflection. ]***

> [!Quote] ***[ Failure is not punished. It is consumed... but a True Performance is acknowledged. ]***
