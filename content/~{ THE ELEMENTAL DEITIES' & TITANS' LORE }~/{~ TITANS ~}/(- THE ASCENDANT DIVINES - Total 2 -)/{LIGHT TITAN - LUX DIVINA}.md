### **~||LIGHT TITAN : LUX DIVINA||~**
***{Creator: LIGHT RADIANCE/RADIANTIA LUCIS}***
*-The Titan of the Divine Light-*

*   ***FORM:*** A humanoid figure composed entirely of pure, condensed divine light.
*   ***SIZE:*** The smallest of all Titans; approximately 2.1× larger than an average Elementian.
*   ***STATUS:*** The only Titan unaffected by the Infection.

* **(TRAITS)**
    *   ***ABSOLUTE SUPREMACY:*** The strongest, fastest, most durable, and most powerful of the Titans.
    *   ***INTANGIBLE DIVINITY:*** Movements are fluid and divine; can phase through physical matter at will.
    *   ***RADIANT PEACE:*** Emits no hostility unless provoked; its presence passively heals minor wounds of allies.
    *   ***LIGHT-SPEED REFLEXES:*** Capable of reacting to threats before they manifest.

*Graceful, kind, calm, and unwavering.*

> [!Quote] ***[ Lux Divina does not dominate...it illuminates. ]***
