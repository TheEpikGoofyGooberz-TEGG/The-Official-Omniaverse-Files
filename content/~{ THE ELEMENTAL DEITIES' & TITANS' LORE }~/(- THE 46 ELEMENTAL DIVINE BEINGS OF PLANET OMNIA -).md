# <{~THE 46 DIVINE BEINGS: THE ARCHITECTS OF REALITY~}>

The Pantheon of 46 comprises the 23 primordial Deities and their 23 Titanic manifestations. They represent the active enforcement of the concepts held within the Heart of Omnia.

———————————————————————————————————

### **<(I. THE 23 DEITIES | THE CONCEPT-BEARERS)>**

The Deities are the literal creators of the concepts they bear (e.g., the concept of Fire, Time, Space, etc.).

* **DIVINE PROTECTION:** They are granted a permanent "Immunity Field" by the Heart of Omnia. This field renders them completely impervious to any force—internal or external—that operates within the bounds of the narrative.
* **THE HEART’S PROXY:** They are the conduits through which the Heart regulates the narrative.

> [!WARNING] **THE OMNI-POWER PROTOCOL**
> If the Heart decides to "Omni-Power" these 46 beings, the resulting surge of energy is effectively "GGs" (Game Over) for the entirety of Fiction. These Beings become Omni-versions of themselves. (e.g., Omni-Ignilith)

---

### **<(II. THE 23 TITANS | THE PHYSICAL MANIFESTATIONS)>**

Each 23 Elemental Titans act as the 50% physical manifestations of Each of the 23 Elemental Deities' elements.

* **SCALE OF DESTRUCTION:** They are gargantuan in size (some of them are really smaller than their siblings though). A coordinated attack by all 23 Titans is sufficient to collapse and erase 23 Omniverses.
* **THE IMMORTALITY LOOP:** The Titans are bound by an absolute restoration clause:
    * Even if a Titan is completely wiped from existence, deleted from the narrative, or atomized, they **cannot stay dead**.
    * **THE RESTORATION CONDITION:** As long as their corresponding Deity remains alive, the Titan will continuously reconstitute itself, returning to existence regardless of the weapon used to delete them.
